Why Daylight Saving Time?

From the essay

We want daylight after work, but we also need to get up, travel and send children to school in the morning. Under daylight saving time, clocks run an hour ahead of standard time. Turning them back puts that hour of light back in the morning. In the United States, federal law sets the seasonal schedule where daylight saving is observed. To end the changes nationwide, we'd have to choose between more evening light and earlier winter sunrises.

By Elijah Fernandez, MSW, PMP, CISSP

Elijah Fernandez tracks how therapy records pass through clinics, insurers and outside firms. Some notes have extra protection. He explains how clinics can check who opens files and tell patients where records go. He also lists questions that patients can ask before they seek care.

The True Cost of A Used Car

By Jacob Williams

Jacob Williams explains what a dealer pays before a used car reaches the lot. Auctions, transport, repairs and loans all affect the final profit. He follows the estimates behind a buying limit and shows why waiting for a better offer can add to the costs.

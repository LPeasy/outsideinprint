A dark allegory of memory, comfort, and what returns when a flock forgets the shepherd

By Robert V. Ussley

The shepherd sets out salt and checks the water. Then he vanishes. The flock goes back to grazing. Over time the sheep forget how he kept the wolves away.

“He had walked the boundary lines. He had checked the water.”

“The shepherd was gone.”

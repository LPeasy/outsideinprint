# Layout Ownership Matrix

This document maps layout ownership for each public route family in the repo.

It separates four concerns that are easy to conflate in this codebase:

1. Route and template selection: config, content path, and Hugo lookup rules decide which template runs.
2. Markup ownership: route templates and partials decide which wrappers, sections, and utility classes exist in the DOM.
3. Visual layout ownership: `assets/css/main.css` decides width, centering, spacing, grid behavior, and most geometry.
4. Dead or under-defined hooks: classes that remain in markup but still do not carry enough explicit layout responsibility.

Dashboard publishing is paused and the dashboard build surface has been removed from this repo.

## Ownership Vocabulary

- `global shell`: applies to most or all routes and constrains the outer page frame.
- `shared partial`: shared markup used by multiple route families; a route assembles it but does not fully own it.
- `route-owned`: the route family has a dedicated template and/or dedicated CSS namespace that materially defines layout.
- `dead/under-defined hook`: a class or wrapper exists in markup, but ownership is still too weak to count as explicit layout control.

## Findings

- Layout geometry is still centralized in `assets/css/main.css`, but the cleanup removed several misleading route hooks and promoted real owners in their place.
- Single-page article ownership is now intentionally centered on `piece*`, `piece-fleuron`, `piece-title-block`, `piece-media-plate*`, `piece-record-rail*`, `article-publication-record*`, `studio-sample-exit*`, `newsletter-signup--article-exit`, `journey-links--article-exit`, and the expanded `reading-path*` namespace. The dead generic wrappers `single-page`, `single-page--imported`, `single-content`, and the article-level `running-header*` output were removed, and the separate mounted collection-membership block is no longer part of the active article flow.
- The article-exit continuation zone now owns `reading-path__header`, `reading-path__actions`, `reading-path__preview`, `reading-path__preview-item`, and `reading-path__archive-links` as explicit route-level hooks.
- Articles with complete `studio_sample` front matter keep the publication record and replace the standard newsletter, reading-path, signup, and journey-link exit stack with `layouts/partials/article/studio-sample-exit.html`.
- `/archive/` owns the canonical long-form archive shell through `layouts/archive/list.html` and the `essays-front*` namespace.
- `/essays/` and `/syd-and-oliver/` are compatibility redirects. The canonical dialogue collection is `/collections/syd-and-oliver-dialogues/`, while the dedicated Syd and Oliver feed remains at `/syd-and-oliver/index.xml`.
- Collections, Gallery, and Library now share a shallow `section-front*` opening shell that borrows the Archive route's smoked-paper framing without inheriting archive-specific month/year structure.
- Collections now own their inner structure explicitly through the `collections-broadsheet*` directory, neutral `collection-record*` rows, and the collection-detail `collection-section*` namespace. The visible collection pages no longer mount the old `collection-progress*` panel, visited-row hooks, card-grid directory, or per-collection room skin.
- `/about/` explains the imprint through the reader's interests: sustained attention, its subject territory and forms, evidence-led independence, and a contributor invitation. `section-front--about` and `about-route*` retain the shared visual style; `.about-route__actions` provides 44px newsletter and featured-reading links. Author information is secondary. The page reuses `newsletter_signup.html` with the `about_newsletter` source slot and `about-newsletter` anchor, plus shared `journey_links` navigation and `piece-body` typography. Signup copy stays plain-language newsletter copy; bookstore promotion is not part of this route's body.
- The homepage moves through six distinct regions: the stats-only `home_reader_banner.html` partial, a compact feed-escape reader note with imprint and author paths, the five-piece `home-v2-featured` reading grid, `home-v2-library` navigation, the `home_reader_newsletter.html` signup partial, and a button-only `home-v2-next` region linking to `/contribute/`. The stats wrapper retains `aria-label="Outside In Print at a glance"`. The library navigation uses `aria-label="Keep reading"` and contains only Browse the library (`/library/`) and Surprise me (`/random/`); Archive remains in the shared Read navigation and at `/archive/`. `home-front-page__welcome-label`, `home-front-page__welcome-body`, `home-front-page__welcome-copy`, and `home-front-page__welcome-links` own the note. `assets/js/home-reader-note.js` enhances the complete default text with a mobile-only disclosure; the native button owns the accessible expanded state. `data/homepage_metrics.yaml` owns all banner and per-piece figures; see `docs/homepage-metrics.md` for definitions and refresh rules.
- `home_reader_newsletter.html` owns the direct Buttondown signup after library navigation. It retains the `home-reader-banner*` signup/form hooks, `home-reader-banner-title` heading ID, `home-reader-email` input ID, provider gating, field and privacy behavior, and `homepage_reader_banner` analytics source slot. Its `home-reader-newsletter` wrapper distinguishes the signup region from the opening stats. The final `home-v2-next` region uses `aria-label="Become a contributor"` and contains only the existing 44px `home-v2-next__cta` link to `/contribute/`. The wrapper centers the button with `display:flex` and `justify-content:center`, uses a `.5rem` top margin, and has no border or background. The former article, eyebrow, heading, and paragraph are absent; the contributor page remains unchanged.
- The Featured Articles region owns one `home-v2-featured__lead`, automatically chosen from the newest eligible publication, and four `home-v2-featured__item` cards inside `home-v2-featured__supporting`. The supporting editorial order is The Dolphin Company (`/essays/the-dolphin-company/`), What I Had (`/syd-and-oliver/what-i-had/`), Default Owner (`/essays/default-owner/`), then Reverse Origami (`/essays/reverse-origami/`); duplicate or unavailable picks use eligible newest-first fallback to retain five unique pieces. Reverse Origami resolves from `content/essays/musings/reverse-origami.md`. The Dolphin Company's homepage-only `Case study` label does not change its canonical `Essay` metadata; Dialogue and Musing labels follow existing metadata. None of the four supporting routes has a metric source record, so their cards omit audience badges while historical unfeatured records remain intact. The Bookstore, Almanack teaser, collections strip, and standalone illustration grid are not part of the active homepage composition.
- Supporting cards reuse the approved article `featured_image` and alt text as square artwork at every viewport width. A resolved image adds `home-v2-featured__item--illustrated`; above 768px, `home-v2-featured__item-media` owns the square picture linking to the article, and `home-v2-featured__item-copy` wraps metadata, title, and summary. At 768px and below, supporting cards form one column while each illustrated card retains a 6rem square artwork column beside its copy. `home-v2-featured__image-toggle` owns the mobile artwork trigger outside the metadata. `home_featured_image_button.html` provides a hidden native `button[data-home-featured-image-trigger]` that JavaScript enables and a no-JavaScript `a[data-home-featured-image-fallback]` containing the same artwork and linking directly to the full illustration. Both mobile artwork variants use the shared image model and picture renderer with lazy loading and `sizes="120px"`, as does the desktop picture. `home_featured_image_dialog.html` and `assets/js/home-featured-image.js` own the native dialog opened by tapping mobile artwork, with image-click, close-button, and Escape closing. Titles continue to link to articles. The lead retains its existing wide hero; shared image rendering continues to own sources and responsive image output.
- `/authors/robert-v-ussley/` owns a portrait-led, first-person author introduction through `section-front--author` and `author-route*`. The profile's 44px actions lead to Selected Writing and the weekly newsletter. The route presents the existing Selected Writing, shared newsletter signup (`author_newsletter` source slot, `author-newsletter` anchor), deduplicated Recent Writing, then Books. It preserves the factual role line and all book destinations, reuses `journey_links` for further discovery, and keeps shared author summaries in `data/authors.yaml` consistent with the profile.
- `/apps/`, `/apps/bucks-machine/`, and `/apps/baseball-upside-risk/` are public development-preview routes. They own a restrained product interface through the `apps-index`, `apps-card`, `apps-product`, `apps-status`, `apps-packet-preview`, `apps-interpretation`, `apps-snapshot`, `apps-workflow`, `apps-deliverables`, `apps-samples`, `apps-limitations`, `apps-companion`, and `apps-identity` namespaces while retaining the global OIP masthead, footer, theme, and page shells.
- `/shop/` and its three book-detail routes own an editorial storefront through the `bookstore-index`, `bookstore-record*`, `bookstore-product*`, and status-aware offer namespaces. Kindle becomes the emphasized available offer only while every direct EPUB offer remains unavailable; the existing Square data and gating stay intact for later activation.
- Remaining ambiguity is concentrated in `library-group`, which still relies more on shared flow than on a dedicated route-level layout selector.

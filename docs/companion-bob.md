# Sitewide interactive Pet Bob

Website implementation prepared in `codex/pet-bob-live-20261011` from the approved `codex/pet-bob-masthead-20261011` preview, based on main `bd1ef9f0af194eaeb0e7548e230fdc3ada19d1a5`. Publication follows the protected PR and verified Pages deployment workflow.

## Reader behavior

Disclosure is the same on desktop and mobile: a fresh load starts with only Bob's sprite. The automatic greeting bubble, wheel, jukebox and reading field stay hidden. Clicking or tapping Bob reveals the wheel; selecting the wheel opens JUKE-BOB and its controls. Reloads and returning visits start collapsed too. Keyboard users can activate the focused canvas with Enter to reveal and focus the wheel, then press Enter again to open the controls. Internal soft navigation preserves controls the reader has explicitly opened.

Bob uses the actual Idle Times Pet renderer and interaction controllers. His default home is the masthead on the homepage and inner pages, with reserved space beside the wordmark on desktop and phones. The homepage starts with A note to the reader beneath the masthead and navigation; the Meet Idle Bob promotion and Steam card are removed. Visit recognition continues silently without an automatic greeting animation or bubble.

Bob docks in the lower-right corner when his starting slot scrolls away. Pick him up and drag him to choose a free position over the page. He uses the desktop pet's hanging, swing and landing behavior, then stays where placed while the reader scrolls. His position is saved as viewport proportions and restored across reloads and internal navigation, clamped to the available screen after resizing.

JUKE-BOB opens with a compact reading field beneath Bob and the player. The gear sits to its left and opens Sound, Home, Browse albums, Activity, Help and Hide. The website's former Talk control is replaced by the reading feature described below. Right-clicking Bob toggles the jukebox and menu open or closed while music keeps playing. Home returns Bob to his masthead slot (or the default corner while that slot is offscreen), closing the controls. Hide removes Bob and his controls, pauses his animation and sound effects, and remembers the choice across navigation and reloads; music continues through the shared player. Active compact music controls remain at the safe bottom-right corner. The quiet Show Bob button restores him with controls collapsed and keyboard focus on the canvas. With the canvas focused, arrow keys move Bob, Shift increases the step, Home restores the default placement, and Enter reveals and focuses the wheel; Enter on the wheel opens settings. Activity offers three mutually exclusive modes: Excited, Relaxed and Calm. Calm holds a still pose; choosing Excited or Relaxed resumes the idle cycle at that pace. The Sound credits link is removed from Bob's controls; the packaged credits file remains available with the exported assets.

This includes the Pet routines, cursor interactions, props, music and settings. It does not include the desk view, illustration unlocks, comic rewards, publishing/economy progression or Steam integration. Music waits for Play. Greetings remain silent. Browser audio rules require interaction before Pet sound effects can play.

Menus and ordinary dialogs temporarily conceal Bob and pause his visual interactions while music continues. Closing them restores the same Pet controls and reading choices. Background tabs stop the animation loop but keep music playing. The Paper-Bob arcade pauses music at its current position and resumes prior play intent when Bob returns. All these cases retain the existing player and unlocked sound-effects context. Transparent areas outside Bob and his visible controls let the reader interact with the webpage.

Internal site navigation now replaces the page content while keeping Bob’s iframe connected. Music, controls, reading choices and Pet state survive ordinary page changes and Back/Forward; greetings do not restart. Following a Bob recommendation title or its preview's **Read this piece** link closes his controls and reading bubble, clears saved placement, and returns him to the destination masthead home on desktop and mobile. Music and conversation history survive soft navigation. Reloading, leaving the site, opening another tab, or falling back to a full page load starts a new runtime. With storage blocked, Bob remains usable and persists through soft navigation, but saved preferences may not survive a reload. Clearing browser storage resets visit recognition.

## Shared album player

`data/music.json` is the website-owned album source. It generates the Music page metadata and the runtime's version-1 `music/playlist.json`, preserving the original eight track IDs alongside the seven supplied Sans Serif tracks. Sans Serif appears first; a fresh player selects Chairman, while a valid saved selection is retained. The page initially browses Sans Serif without changing the playing album. Browsing never fetches audio, and neither page entry nor reload autoplays. Play album begins its first track; track selection starts the selected track. Navigation and automatic advancement stay within the playing album.

The cabinet, `/music/` page and compact brass/cream/green controls share the persistent iframe's single audio instance. The website-only `window.oipBobMusic` interface exposes `getState()`, `subscribe(callback)`, `playAlbum(id)`, `playTrack(id)`, `play()`, `pause()`, `togglePlay()`, `next()`, `previous()` and `setVolume(value)`. Same-origin page controls call playback directly within the trusted click stack. Readiness, buffering, actual playing state and playback errors come from the provider; a pending or rejected `play()` is not presented as successful playback. The lazy controller can initialize while Bob is hidden. `music-ready` announces the interface, and subscriptions keep all surfaces synchronized.

Compact controls appear after playback has actually started. Pausing there retains them. An open cabinet hides them; explicitly closing the cabinet while playing shows them again, while explicitly closing it paused dismisses them until playback resumes. There is no separate dismiss button. `cabinet-state` distinguishes `user`, `home` and `hide` transitions so navigation, Home and temporary concealment do not manufacture an explicit paused dismissal. The cabinet's Browse albums action sends `browse-music` to the parent without opening another player. The existing arcade suspension/resume behavior remains separate from Hide.

## Reading suggestions and free discovery

`assets/js/companion-bob-reading.js` owns the reading conversation, collection discovery, speech bubble and article previews. `assets/css/companion-bob-reading.css` supplies the compact glass presentation. The iframe owns the composer and hit regions; messages on the existing same-origin channel carry `reading-query`, `reading-layout`, `reading-discover`, configuration, pending state, focus/edit requests and reserved space. Article previews support hover, keyboard focus and an explicit touch button. Very narrow screens place the bubble above the combined Bob/player area.

The checked-in `[params.bob_reading]` configuration is disabled with an empty `api_base`. This shows **Explore collections**, which needs no API service. Enabling AI requires both `enabled = true` and an explicit HTTPS API origin; HTTP loopback is accepted only for loopback review pages. The enabled field uses the placeholder **What would you like to read about?**, with **New subject** available before any AI request. Neither mode opens a welcome bubble. No provider credential belongs in the site or runtime.

New subject cancels pending work and offers three distinct actual collections. Selecting one samples three canonical pieces from its membership; Shorter, Longer and More remain local. More excludes previously shown pieces and can offer a final partial batch. An explicit empty AI result or an exhausted local filter shows **No exact matches…** and three new collection choices. API service-disable and budget-limit responses offer collections with an honest explanation. Clarification, scope redirection, malformed responses and transport failures retain their distinct states; retryable errors offer explicit Retry and Edit request. No failure silently issues another paid request.

AI requests use `/api/bob/status` and `/api/bob/recommend` at the configured origin. Conversation preferences, at most eight history messages, seen IDs and current choices remain in memory. Payloads are bounded to 16 KiB; pending requests have a 25-second client deadline. Retrying preserves the failed request. A `catalog_changed` response invalidates the static cache and removes its stale version from an explicit retry. New subject resets all conversation state. Status configuration indicates service readiness, not a successful upstream probe.

Hugo generates `/companion-bob/catalog.json` through `layouts/partials/bob/catalog.html` and `hero.html` during the same release as the pages. It joins published Library entries to public collection membership, excludes draft/future/expired/noindex entries, and uses each article's actual rendered hero (or Modern Bio portrait). Image-less pieces remain valid. Canonical URLs retain `https://outsideinprint.org`; local link navigation uses the same path. A content hash supplies `catalogVersion`, so new publications and metadata updates require no manually maintained snapshots. See [the Worker cost and release plan](bob-reading-cost-plan.md) for server configuration and limits.

## Ownership and runtime

The site parent lives in `assets/js/companion-bob.js`. It owns visit recognition, position modes (`masthead`, `docked`, `free`, `hidden`), the speech bubble, browser storage and page-overlay coordination. `companion-bob-visit.js` handles versioned visit/preferences records. `oip-bob-position:v1` stores only normalized x/y placement, with no user identity or server request.

The shared masthead reserves one slot on every page without mounting a second pet. `layouts/partials/companion_bob.html` creates a transparent viewport iframe, loading fallback, greeting and Show Bob control. The shared base and custom 404 template include it. Readers open controls through Bob's native gear or right-click; the separate Spend time with Bob button has been removed.

The iframe at `/companion-bob/runtime/` is a full-viewport rendering surface with no visible container. Its CSS clip path is the union of Bob's actual sprite hit bands and visible control rectangles, supplied by the same hit-region computation used for the native desktop window. This gives real browser hit-test pass-through outside those regions. The frame starts fully clipped and inert until drawing and regions are ready. All cross-frame messages require the exact source window, same origin and `oip-site-bob-v1` channel.

Parent-to-child messages cover foot-anchor placement, visibility, theme, passive cursor position, greetings and opening settings. The runtime reports readiness, painted state, hit regions, user position changes, activity, Home, Hide and errors. Only a settled user move is persisted. Temporary panel fitting does not overwrite the saved position.

The parent also sends `viewport` with `{width,height,left,top,scale}` from its visual viewport in layout-viewport CSS pixels. Runtime composer, cabinet fitting and hit-region clipping use those bounds; reported rectangles already use that same coordinate system. The actual iframe textarea is 16px, with a 48px composer and 44px input/send targets. While the mobile/coarse-pointer input is focused, `reading-typing` temporarily conceals the cabinet/settings and the parent's reading popups, leaving the composer above the visible viewport bottom. This does not toggle the logical cabinet, stop music or persist a new Bob position. Blur restores the previous layout. Pinch zoom remains enabled. Actual iPhone Safari keyboard, rotation and pinch behavior requires device validation; phone-sized Chromium evidence alone cannot establish it.

The 9,204-byte static `idle.webp` fallback is drawn from original artwork. Its existing art manifest/build script retain derivation provenance. The earlier compact-only renderer is no longer used. The previous standalone Pet demo under `static/demos/idle-times-pet/` remains available independently; it is not mounted by the site companion.

## Persistent navigation

`assets/js/site-navigation.js` uses self-hosted Swup 4.10.0 (MIT; provenance and license under `assets/js/vendor/`). It swaps `#site-page` and `#site-overlays`; Bob, his greeting and Show Bob control remain outside both. Swup handles link interception, fetches, browser history and navigation cancellation. The integration follows Hugo aliases and Random routes, handles Library query-only history, restores scroll, moves keyboard focus, announces the new page, updates route metadata and analytics config, and limits cached pages to 20.

`oip:before-route` runs while old content exists. Widgets remove listeners, close viewers, destroy the separate Paper-Bob game and cancel or ignore stale requests. The router installs the new markup, executes marked widget scripts once, then emits `oip:route`. `route-widget-lifecycle.js` remounts each registered widget. The companion rebinds the new masthead slot and controls without replacing its iframe. Library filters use `oipNavigation.updateQuery()` so their URLs retain navigation history metadata. Analytics closes the previous reading interval and records one page view for the destination.

A failed fetch, incompatible persistent asset fingerprint, unsupported script or missing page container falls back to normal document navigation. External links, modified clicks, downloads and standalone demos keep native browser behavior. New templates with interactive scripts must register a route widget and mark their script with a stable `data-oip-route-script` key. Full document loads remain the no-JavaScript fallback. No form submission or checkout is intercepted.

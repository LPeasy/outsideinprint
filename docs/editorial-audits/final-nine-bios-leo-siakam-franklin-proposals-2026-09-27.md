## 3. Benjamin Franklin: exact short repairs, no broad historical rewrite

File: `content/essays/benjamin-franklin-how-americas-funniest-founder-made-greatness-feel-possible.md`.

### Exact corrections proposed

1. **National age:** `The U.S. was barely four years old.` → `The U.S. was nearly fourteen years old.` April 1790 is just short of fourteen years after July 1776. Preserve the paragraph.
2. **Founding documents, both occurrences:** body `He helped draft both the Declaration of Independence and the Constitution.` → `He helped draft the Declaration of Independence and participated in the Constitutional Convention.` List `Helped draft both the Declaration of Independence and the Constitution` → `Helped draft the Declaration of Independence and participated in the Constitutional Convention`. [National Archives biography](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/founding-fathers-pennsylvania).
3. **Library:** `America's first public lending library` → `America's first subscription library`. The [Autobiography](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/148/148-h/148-h.htm) describes the subscription model; retain the existing Library Company label.
4. **Collaborative institutions:** `He founded libraries, hospitals, fire departments, and the postal system.` → `He helped found libraries, hospitals, and fire companies, and helped develop the postal system.` The Autobiography records collaborators and specific institutions; [USPS](https://about.usps.com/who/profile/history/list-of-postmasters-general.htm) confirms the appointment, not creation of all postal service by one person.
5. **Arrival narrative:** remove only `and a loaf of bread under his arms` from the running-away sentence. The bread was bought after reaching Philadelphia. Then replace the stitched quotation `dirty from being so long in the boat, my pockets with rolls, and a roll under each arm.` with the actual short excerpt `walk'd off with a roll under each arm, and eating the other.` Preserve its bold block and the author's following interpretation. The immediately preceding introduction becomes `He writes in his Autobiography that he` instead of `that he arrived`, so the exact quotation fits grammatically. Link the Autobiography's arrival passage. Existing modernization note permits the straight apostrophe; do not invent a pockets-of-rolls quotation.
6. **Modesty quotation:** replace only the unsupported quotation beginning `I was generally a leader in measures` with the verified short ending `I should probably be proud of my humility.` This preserves the self-mocking point and surrounding paragraph. The Autobiography's pride passage explicitly frames this as a hypothetical failure to escape pride, not a boast. If the owner wants the original longer sentence retained, request its edition/page instead; current retrieval did not locate it.
7. **Moral-perfection splice:** `at any time, but I` → `at any time… But I`. There is omitted text between the two existing clauses in the Autobiography. Keep the rest and the existing modernization caveat.
8. **Epitaph:** `But the Work shall not be lost,` → `But the Work shall not be wholly lost,`. Link the [full Library of Congress transcription](https://www.loc.gov/exhibits/franklin/bf-trans61.html). It includes the closing “Corrected and amended” line, so that line stays. Existing spelling/punctuation modernization can remain explicitly covered by the editor's note; do not replace the entire poem.
9. **Penny attribution:** delete only `A penny saved is a penny earned.` and its now-unnecessary forced line break. Retain the adjacent “Well done” saying, verified in the [public-domain Reid edition, page 201](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/36151/pg36151-images.html). Change its setup `He gave us lines like:` → `He gave us lines like this:` so the one remaining example reads cleanly. [Franklin Institute](https://fi.edu/en/science-and-education/benjamin-franklin/7-things-benjamin-franklin-never-said) identifies the penny-earned form as a misquotation; do not add another maxim merely to maintain two lines.
10. **Resource label only:** `Audio version (LibriVox): Listen on Archive.org` → `Scanned edition: Read on Archive.org`. Keep its destination and the other resources. The leading headphone emoji can be changed to the existing book style only if included in this approval; it is not an audio file.

## Corrections and Analysis Boundaries

- The original “sinkholes” claim is replaced with measured land subsidence, including in the description.
- The 82/12/6 figures are historical portfolio shares, not current tap-water composition. The old 1940–2017 image is retained as historical artwork, not evidence for the 2020 percentages.
- “Three pillars” and “four traditions” are now clearly the author's comparison, not the plan's formal five guiding principles or official taxonomy.
- Approximate decadal long-range updates are distinguished from annual progress reporting. No unverified five-to-ten-year mandate remains.
- Precaution can reduce exposure but cannot make the city almost immune. The adaptive/scenario conclusion is an analytical inference, not a demonstrated causal estimate of saved gallons.
- Unqualified 2030s completion, an 11-million-acre-foot forecast without a model/horizon, an unbounded 13–14-million long-term mean, economic value without a defined valuation method, and speculative Hoover shutdown timing were removed. They are not required for the argument and were not dressed in plausible citations.
- Reservoir conditions, city population, utility portfolio shares, and river-wide service totals have distinct dates and denominators. The essay names those distinctions instead of silently updating them to 2026.

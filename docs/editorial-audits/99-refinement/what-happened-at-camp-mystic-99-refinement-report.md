## Source Hierarchy Notes

The governing new source is the Texas Legislature's [Report on the Camp Mystic Flood Disaster of July 4, 2025](https://www.house.texas.gov/pdfs/committees/355/Report-on-the-Camp-Mystic-Flood-Disaster-of-July-4-2025.pdf?d=06182026). Its narrative documents the July 3 Flood Watch, the 1:14 a.m. July 4 Flash Flood Warning for Hunt, weather monitoring and internal communications at Camp Mystic, water entering Look Inn around 2:20 a.m., the gate house flooding around 2:55 a.m., and the decision to begin evacuating cabins around 3:00 a.m. The report also records that the warning reached multiple people at camp, that no protocol required a response to it, and that the public-address system was not used for an evacuation announcement.

The report states that its purpose is to present a legislative timeline and context for further legislation, not to resolve governmental, corporate, or individual responsibility in civil disputes. The revised essay must therefore attribute contested conclusions to the committees and must not recast the report as a court judgment.

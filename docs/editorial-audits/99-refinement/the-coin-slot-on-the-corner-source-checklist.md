## Claim-to-source corrections

- Posted notices: [47 CFR 64.703(b)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-64/subpart-G/section-64.703), substantive official indexed text read, specifies provider identity, rates, and complaints. Former 64.706 citation concerned emergency routing, not notices. Corrected destination and description.
- Compensation: [47 USC 276](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/47/276), statutory text read, separates competition/compensation and public-interest phones. [FCC 18-21](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-18-21A1.pdf), official indexed paragraphs 1-3 read, removes audit/reporting requirements, changes certification, and removes expired compensation provisions. The essay no longer presents that order as a general public-access deregulation.
- Free emergency access: [47 CFR 64.1330(b), 2023 official compilation](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2023-title47-vol3/pdf/CFR-2023-title47-vol3-part64.pdf), indexed regulatory passage read. The state duty covers free dial tone, emergency calls, and relay calls. This replaces the inaccessible eCFR destination without claiming a newly adopted rule.
- Kiosk functions: [LinkNYC FAQ](https://www.link.nyc/faq.html), general services and phone-calling instructions read. Built-in keypad/tablet calling does not require a personal phone; Wi-Fi does. Corrected the essay's contrary implication. Operator description is evidence of offered functionality, not an independent uptime guarantee.
- Mobile emergencies: [911.gov FAQ](https://www.911.gov/calling-911/frequently-asked-questions/), no-active-plan passage read. Removed active-account and unlocked-phone prerequisites; described callback/location limitations without implying a powered phone guarantees connectivity.
- NYC transition: [FOX 5's May 23, 2022 on-scene report](https://www.fox5ny.com/news/last-nyc-payphone-being-removed), event details and remaining-phone exceptions read. Replaced an unavailable city announcement and overbroad last-city-owned wording with the specific removal and exceptions. This reporting supports the ceremony, not the regulatory analysis.
- Gray patent: [US408709A](https://patents.google.com/patent/US408709A/en), specification and date read; retained narrow coin-controlled mechanism claim.
- Privacy: [LinkNYC policy](https://www.link.nyc/privacy-policy.html), technical-information, retention, sharing, and camera sections read. Retained general data-governance discussion; no claim of universal tracking or sale of personal information added.

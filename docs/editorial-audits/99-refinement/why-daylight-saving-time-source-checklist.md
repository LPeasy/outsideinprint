## Source hierarchy and limitations

Primary legal records establish rules; contemporary debate and presidential statements establish arguments by named actors. Garfield and other officials are interested sources for their predictions. The two 2008 research estimates have different exposures and denominators. Secondary reporting was used only in prior discovery and is not substituted for these claims. Advocacy interpretations are not adopted as measured effects. No unresolved historical source gap blocks this bounded account. Any time gap between this package and actual release requires a fresh legislative-status look if a new relevant action is known; do not repeat the full history or image review without a change.

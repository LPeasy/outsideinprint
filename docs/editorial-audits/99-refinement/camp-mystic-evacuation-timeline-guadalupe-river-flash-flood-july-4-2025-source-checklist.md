## Claim Decisions

- **Early spokesperson accounts:** Attribute the 2:00/2:30 estimates to ABC's report of Carr. ABC explicitly called the estimate preliminary and reported the change. Removed the implication that ABC presented an independently established chronology or necessarily failed to question Carr.
- **Disclosed later record:** Added the June 2026 legislative findings, explicitly dated in the public text and September 27 revision history. Preserved the 2025 publication date. The inquiry's p. 6 limit on adjudicating civil responsibility is linked.
- **Actual camp chronology:** pp. 79-83 place warning awareness by 1:20, impassable Edmunson Creek by 2:01, water in Look Inn about 2:20, evacuation decision about 3:00, and photographed movement at 3:23/3:26. None establishes every camper's individual departure time.
- **Geography:** pp. 54-56 distinguish the Guadalupe campus, Flats, Senior Hill, and separate Cypress Lake camp. Removed unsupported highest-ground and dining-hall elevation claims. The 2011 flood map and later amendments are explained without resolving LOMA legality.
- **Probability calculation:** `1 - 0.99^30` is about 0.2603. The text now states constant-probability and independence assumptions and does not treat that arithmetic as a measured cabin risk.
- **Evacuation arithmetic:** `500 / 1 / 60 = 8.33` minutes is an illustrative walking calculation only. Removed the unsupported 400-500 population estimate, 20-40 minute mobilization, universal 1 ft/sec planning value, 300-700 foot route range, assumed sequential waves, and 30-70 minute clearance conclusion. No peer-reviewed operational model was supplied or found supporting those site-specific values.
- **Flood threshold inference:** Withdrawn: Hunt 15/25/32 foot stages establish when particular cabins flood. The essay has no surveyed datum reconciliation or hydraulic model that could support that conversion.
- **Crest timing:** Corrected 4:35 to 5:10 for 37.52 feet, attributed to the official retrospective record. Do not conflate a news graphic's provisional line, a later crest marker, and real-time warning availability.
- **Counterfactual evacuation:** The conclusion that earlier general instructions to evacuate on foot allowed sufficient time is explicitly the committees' C-12/C-14 finding, pp. 105-106; it is not a result of the essay's withdrawn calculation and is not a guarantee that every conceivable route was safe.
- **Practical lessons:** Site-specific drills, assigned duties, and route knowledge are author analysis grounded in the report's deficiencies, not a newly certified emergency plan.

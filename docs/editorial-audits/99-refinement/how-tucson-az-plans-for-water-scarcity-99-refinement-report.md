## Editorial Philosophy Audit

Decision: PASS

| Test | Decision | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence | PASS | Major factual clusters now point to USGS, CAP, Tucson Water's plan and implementation report, city adoption records, ADWR accounting, Census data, and Reclamation. | Logic | PASS | The argument moves from supply dependence and institutional constraints to four explicitly authorial planning lenses. A river allocation is not equated with measured flow, and a conditional scenario is not converted into a forecast. The conclusion offers a useful approach rather than claiming that the framework demonstrably caused all conservation gains. |
| Incentives | PASS | Tiered residential rates create a marginal incentive to conserve; short-run cost control is weighed against early investment and later adjustment. No unsupported quantified price effect or universal rate structure is claimed. |
| Tradeoffs | PASS | Early investment may mean larger bills and stricter conservation; incremental action may leave exposure if conditions change faster than expected. Reuse requires cost, regulatory, demonstration, and public-acceptance work. No framework eliminates all costs or risk. |
| Consequences | PASS | Groundwater depletion is connected to recorded subsidence. The essay traces future reliance on banked water and groundwater, regional supply constraints, and consequences for residents and water users without presenting a speculative shutdown date or deterministic crisis. |
| Uncertainty | PASS | The guarantee-like 2030s completion wording, near-immunity claim, half-empty snapshot, unspecific economic valuation, and unbounded flow average/11-million forecast were removed or qualified. Conditional city scenarios are distinguished from promises. The September 2025 perspective is preserved rather than silently rewritten as current conditions. |
| Institutional Behavior | PASS | City/utility planning and rate incentives are distinguished from state credit rules and interstate/federal supply decisions. Adoption by Mayor and Council and reporting to them and the public are traceable. City population is not confused with the larger utility service area or river-wide beneficiaries. |

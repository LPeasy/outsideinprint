## Editorial Philosophy Audit

Decision: PASS

- Evidence: PASS — Major dates, figures, offers, construction details, and reported outcomes are tied to period reports, filings, releases, photographs, and a clearly attributed contemporary farewell account. The existing source ledger records scope and dating limits.
- Logic: PASS — Tickets, hotel eligibility, airport transport, attractions, and ownership sales support the account of organizing a whole vacation. The draft does not treat promotional language as proof of satisfaction or group differences as a causal estimate.
- Incentives: PASS — Disney's interest in hotel occupancy, park days, guest purchases, and capital recovery is stated concretely. Families decide how long to stay, which extras to buy, and whether to commit money to future holidays.
- Tradeoffs: PASS — Lower daily admission can accompany a larger total trip bill. Complimentary transport depends on hotel eligibility. More accommodation and attractions require capital, while changed nightlife offerings displace experiences some guests wanted to keep.
- Consequences: PASS — The essay follows programs into bookings and eligibility, capital into physical attractions and rooms, and closure decisions into the final-night response. Later occupancy and spending results appear alongside continued building.

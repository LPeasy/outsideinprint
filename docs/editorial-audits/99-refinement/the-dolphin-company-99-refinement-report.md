## Initial blockers and authorized resolutions

### 1. Transfer destinations and corporate ownership

Locations: `Gulf World Closes for Good`, the final transfer paragraph in `Bankruptcy Mechanics ~ Assets on Paper, Animals in Pools`, and `You can see both sides in the paperwork`.

The prior text grouped all recipients within one corporate network. Facility homepages and the unattributed waiver excerpt did not establish that conclusion.

Resolution: [CMA's release](https://mission.cmaquarium.org/news/four-rough-toothed-dolphins-welcomed-to-clearwater-marine-aquarium/) and [FWC's account](https://myfwc.com/about/inside-fwc/le/media-resources/) establish the nonprofit destination; the [company's contemporaneous announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/marineland-dolphin-adventure-and-dolphin-connection-receive-a-total-of-seven-bottlenose-dolphins-from-gulf-world-marine-park-302483129.html) establishes its two park destinations. The article now distinguishes them throughout.

The revision does not infer legal title, security interests, or motives from a care destination. The argument about financial exposure survives as a bounded inference, not a statement that every transfer preserved collateral. It expressly acknowledges that immediate care needs and creditor interests can overlap.

### 2. Mexican proceeding and legal terminology

Locations: the paragraph beginning `The U.S. Chapter 11 filing sat beside a parallel Mexican process`, `The Dolphin Company's collapse was bigger than any single park`, and `The Mexican Quiebra Process`.

The prior text equated quiebra with the general restructuring framework and inferred worldwide court coverage from the group's operating footprint. The general legal explainer was insufficient for those case-specific claims.

Resolution: [Mexico's statute, articles 2-3](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LCM.pdf), supplies the stage distinction. [Contemporaneous audited statements, note 28](https://cdn.jamstockex.com/pd/2025/04/2024-December-31-DCOVE-Audited-Financial-Statements.pdf), supply the attributed historical proceeding account. The article retains the broader entity/jurisdiction question as unconfirmed and does not claim a current Mexican procedural status. The relevant case orders were not retrieved; that limitation is explicit rather than disguised as a case finding.

### 3. Claimed stock listing

Location: `A Mexican Dolphin Dynasty Emerges`, paragraph beginning `The company listed shares on the Mexican stock exchange in 2016`.

The prior parent-company listing assertion had only a homepage link. No matching issuer/listing record was identified in the focused search; absence from that search is not proof that no such record exists.

Resolution: retain the exact subject as explicitly unconfirmed and distinguish the [affiliate's documented listing and control history](https://www.jamstockex.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Annual-Report-2023-Final-Dolphin-Cove.pdf). The global-brand sentence remains a separate observation. No invented attribution or unsupported `reportedly` hedge was substituted.

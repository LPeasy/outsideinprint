## Fresh checks for the evergreen edition

Nassau Cruise Port’s November 5 endpoint returned six calls: Norwegian Getaway, Sapphire Princess, Celebrity Reflection, Norwegian Jewel, Margaritaville At Sea Paradise, and Carnival Spirit. The source supports the four selected vessels and the six-call framing. It does not establish a simultaneous arrival; the essay makes no such claim.

The Revivalists’ announcement identifies Norwegian Jewel, Miami–Nassau, November 3–7, 2026. Pearl Island’s itinerary identifies Elizabeth on Bay Marina, Athol Island Pig Beach, Pearl Island, and the advertised three-hour duration.

The original `www.rooke.se` host did not resolve in the HTTP check. Internet Archive’s availability API returned a June 12, 2026 snapshot with status 200. The Windows browser check showed that the standard archive wrapper did not display the document. Direct replay of the same capture opened the PDF in Chromium. The specifications, interview, and cost links point to PDF pages 25, 42, and 46. The research tool also returned the original indexed transcription. Its specification gives 880 passengers, an October 5, 1970 delivery, and a November 7 maiden voyage. The interview passage attributes a $16 million construction cost to Wilhelmsen. No figure changed.

Several archival and official sites returned automated-access restrictions. These results are recorded as restrictions rather than broken citations. FRASER timed out in the bounded request and opened through the research tool. See `link-checks.json` for the exact results and follow-up record.

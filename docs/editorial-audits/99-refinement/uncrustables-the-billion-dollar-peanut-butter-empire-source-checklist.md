## Qualifications and Removed Assertions

- The headline is preserved, but the opening and business section now identify the billion-dollar figure as the historical target. Later outcomes are outside this essay's time frame.
- The undated brand-history page gives a Scottsville date inconsistent with the company's contemporary [February 18, 2005 results](https://investors.jmsmucker.com/news/news-details/2005/The-J.-M.-Smucker-Company-Announces-Third-Quarter-Results-02-18-2005/default.aspx), which place commencement in the first quarter of fiscal 2005. This revision uses dated facility announcements and the supported sequence, not that history-page date.
- Removed the possible shelf-stable format from the business forecast. No source read here establishes a launch plan within the March 2025 frame. Later refrigerated-storage announcements are not shelf-stability evidence and were not imported into the historical narrative.
- Replaced sweeping claims about athletes' reasons for eating the product and consumers' nostalgia with a documented example and a plainly stated interpretation. No performance or health claim follows from a sports endorsement.
- No precise sales contribution is assigned to social media, new flavors, advertising, or any one facility. Company sales reports alone do not identify those causal shares.
- Convenience as a competitive advantage, the costs of scale, brand-extension risk, and the conditional conclusion remain the author's business analysis. They are not presented as findings of an independent market study.

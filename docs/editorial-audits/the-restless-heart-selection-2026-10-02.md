# The Restless Heart: collection selection and launch

Date: 2026-10-02

Status: Owner-authorized collection launch. Six published essays are assigned to the new series. The prayer follow-up will be prepared as a draft after the collection is live.

## Scope

Reviewed the 19 published essay entries explicitly assigned to `moral-religious-philosophical-essays`, including the newly approved publication of *All Is a Gift*. The selection concerns editorial fit, not a new certification of every theological or historical claim in the archive.

The owner broadened the scope to include Christian moral reflections. The Restless Heart should gather essays that explain Catholic teaching or develop a Christian reflection on practicing the faith: prayer, human dignity, conscience, love, discipleship, sacrifice, and the freedom to serve God. Catholic social teaching and Christian moral applications belong alongside spiritual formation. Religious imagery or incidental Christian references alone do not establish a fit.

## Implemented collection arrangement

The exact title is **The Restless Heart**, with a dedicated series identity and explicit membership. The six selected essays move from Moral, Religious, and Philosophical Essays into this series so that their article endings lead into the new reading collection. *Whose Yes* retains Simple Logic as its second membership. The broader topic retains the other thirteen essays.

Collection description: "Catholic teaching and Christian moral reflections on prayer, love, conscience, sacrifice, and the freedom to serve God."

Series slug: `the-restless-heart`. Starting essay: `in-the-image-of-god`. Related collections are Moral, Religious, and Philosophical Essays; Simple Logic; and Syd and Oliver Dialogues. The broader topic now starts with *Consent: From Permission to Sanctity* and links back to this series.

The starter introduces dignity and love through Catholic social teaching. The remaining essays retain the site's standard reading order. *All Is a Gift* and the forthcoming prayer follow-up develop spiritual formation; *Whose Yes*, *The Last Victim*, and *Outside the Garden* develop applications in promises, forgiveness, and social responsibility.

No archival essay body, title, date, version, or source link changes in this launch. The membership changes are front-matter only. The series reuses the approved *All Is a Gift* illustration and receives a distinct heart printer's mark and warm color identity.

## Gauge evidence

The [USGS explanation of gage datum](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/why-doesnt-usgs-measure-gage-height-bottom-stream), read in this review, states that each gage uses its own selected reference point. That site relationship needs documented observations or a supported model; subtracting two unrelated elevations cannot supply it.

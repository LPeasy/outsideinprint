## 2. The three price bullets

Replace the existing bread, new-home and college bullets with:

- In January 1980, white pan bread averaged about $0.50 per pound; in January 2023, it averaged about $1.89 ([BLS](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/APU0000702111)).
- The median sales price of new U.S. homes rose from $64,600 in 1980 to $428,600 in 2023 ([Census/HUD](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/MSPNHSUSA)).
- Average undergraduate tuition and required fees rose from $1,289 in 1980–81 to $14,688 in 2022–23, in current dollars ([NCES](https://nces.ed.gov/programs/digest/d23/tables/dt23_330.10.asp)).

The BLS observations are $0.501 and $1.888 per pound, rounded to cents above. They are January observations, not annual averages or prices for the same physical loaf. The home figures are annual medians, not January-only observations. Tuition is the all-institutions, current-dollar, tuition-and-required-fees column: not room/board or net cost after aid. NCES notes a small institutional-classification change before 1996. No percentage increase is inferred from a quality-adjusted price index.

## 3. Necessary qualification immediately below those bullets

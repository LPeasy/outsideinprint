Details before you inquire

Source limits and exclusions

What you bring

  • One recording, interview, or presentation up to {{ $recordingLimit }} minutes
  • One transcript up to {{ lang.FormatNumber 0 $transcriptLimit }} words
  • One working draft that fits the written scope
  • Or one source packet up to {{ $packetLimit }} pages

What the sprint does not include

  • More interviews
  • Major new research beyond your material and basic fact-checking
  • Custom artwork
  • Website design or coding
  • Long-term social media work or content plans
  • Book-length projects
  • More than one revision round
  • A promise that Outside In Print will publish the essay
How the sprint works

  1. Send the inquiry.

    Tell me what you have, who should read the essay, and what you want it to do. Do not send source files yet.

  2. Agree on the work and book your sprint.

    I decide whether the project is a good fit. If it is, I send a one-page work plan called the written scope. I also send a Square invoice for the {{ $depositPercent }}% deposit.

  3. Get your first draft.

    The {{ $turnaroundDays }}-business-day clock starts after three things happen: you approve the written scope, pay the deposit, and send all agreed source material.

  4. Revise and finish.

    Send all your changes together for the one included revision. Pay the final {{ $finalPercent }}% before I release the final files.

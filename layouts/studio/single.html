The process

From raw material to finished publication.

  1. Send the inquiry.

    Describe the source material, intended reader, and result you need. Do not upload source files yet.

  2. Confirm scope and reserve the sprint.

    Outside In Print confirms fit, sends a one-page scope, and issues a Square invoice for the {{ $depositPercent }}% deposit.

  3. Receive the first draft.

    The first-draft production window begins after the written scope is approved, the deposit is paid, and complete source material is received.

  4. Revise and finish.

    Send one consolidated revision. The final balance is due before release of the final files.

The boundary

What the sprint does not include.

  • Additional interviews
  • Extensive original research beyond supplied material and basic verification
  • Custom illustration
  • Website design or development
  • Ongoing social-media management or content calendars
  • Book-length work
  • More than one revision round
  • Guaranteed publication on Outside In Print

Do not send confidential, classified, privileged, export-controlled, or restricted source material through the public inquiry form.

Questions

Before you inquire.

Can Outside In Print write in my voice?

Yes. The source material anchors the vocabulary, tone, and point of view. The final work remains clear enough for the intended reader.

Who publishes the essay?

Outside In Print delivers publication-ready files for your use. Publication on outsideinprint.org is a separate editorial decision and is not part of the sprint.

What happens to ownership?

The written scope states the rights transferred after full payment. Outside In Print will not use the work as a public example without permission.

What if the project needs more research or another interview?

Outside In Print identifies the added work before the project begins and quotes it separately.

Does preparing or sending the inquiry email reserve a production slot?

No. A slot is reserved only when the written scope is accepted and the deposit is paid.

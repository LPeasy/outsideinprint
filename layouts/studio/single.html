Why it works

The idea is already there.

You do not need more content. Your best ideas are already in the material you have. We find the main point, put the ideas in order, check key facts, and write one finished essay in your voice.

What’s included

One source set. One finished essay.

What you bring

  • One recording, interview, or presentation up to {{ $recordingLimit }} minutes
  • One transcript up to {{ lang.FormatNumber 0 $transcriptLimit }} words
  • One working draft that fits the written scope
  • Or one source packet up to {{ $packetLimit }} pages

Choose one main source set. We can price extra material as separate work.

What you receive

  • One finished essay with your byline, {{ lang.FormatNumber 0 $outputMinimum }}–{{ lang.FormatNumber 0 $outputMaximum }} words
  • A clear title and subtitle
  • An editable file and copy ready for the web
  • A short record of sources and fact checks
  • Three short excerpts you can use to promote the essay
  • One revision round for all your changes

How it works

From source material to a finished essay.

  1. Send the inquiry.

    Tell us what you have, who should read the essay, and what you want it to do. Do not send source files yet.

  2. Agree on the work and book your sprint.

    We decide whether the project is a good fit. If it is, we send a one-page work plan called the written scope. We also send a Square invoice for the {{ $depositPercent }}% deposit.

  3. Get your first draft.

    The {{ $turnaroundDays }}-business-day clock starts after three things happen: you approve the written scope, pay the deposit, and send all agreed source material.

  4. Revise and finish.

    Send all your changes together for the one included revision. Pay the final {{ $finalPercent }}% before we release the final files.

Questions

Before you send an inquiry

Can you write in my voice?

Yes. We use your source material to match your words, tone, and point of view. We also make the essay clear for the people you want to reach.

Who publishes the essay?

We give you a complete finished file set. Outside In Print reserves the right to publish the essay on outsideinprint.org.

What happens to ownership?

The written scope states which rights transfer to you after full payment. Outside In Print keeps the right to publish the finished essay on outsideinprint.org.

What if the project needs more research or another interview?

We tell you about the extra work before the project starts and price it separately.

Does this inquiry reserve a work slot?

No. We reserve a slot only after you accept the written scope and pay the deposit.

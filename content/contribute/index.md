Outside In Print is opening a small contributor lane for original essays and reported articles that reward attention, follow the evidence, and give readers something worth returning to.

## What Fits

We are interested in clear, independent work about public life, institutions, business, risk, culture, technology, and the consequences of how systems are designed. A strong submission makes a specific argument, distinguishes evidence from judgment, and respects uncertainty where it exists.

We are not looking for sponsored posts, search-engine filler, press releases, or partisan talking points.

## Start With a Pitch

Send a short introduction with:

- the working title;
- the question or argument in two or three sentences;
- a draft or a brief outline; and
- a short note about who you are and why you want to write it.

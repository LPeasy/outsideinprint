For a Studio inquiry, review the [Expert-to-Essay Publication Sprint](/studio/#studio-inquiry). The guided form prepares an email to [support@outsideinprint.org](mailto:support@outsideinprint.org); it does not send the message for you. Do not email attachments or source files until Outside In Print requests them.

Email [support@outsideinprint.org](mailto:support@outsideinprint.org) for help with e-book delivery, direct support through Square, a refund, cancellation, privacy request, or storefront policy.

For a Patreon membership or download problem, email the same address. Include your Patreon name, the post title if relevant, and a short description of the problem. Use [Patreon’s official membership help](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/sections/360011948912-Manage-Your-Membership) to manage a membership, or follow its [paid-membership cancellation guide](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/360005502572-Canceling-a-paid-membership).

For factual corrections, editorial questions, rights inquiries, or reprint requests, email [support@outsideinprint.org](mailto:support@outsideinprint.org).

## What to include

For Square transaction help, include the Square order, payment, or subscription identifier and a short description of the problem.

Do not email a full card number, card-security code, password, government identity document, tax credential, or account-recovery code.

Outside In Print ordinarily responds to complete Studio inquiries and commerce-support messages within two business days.

Email [support@outsideinprint.org](mailto:support@outsideinprint.org) for help with EPUB delivery, reader support, a refund, cancellation, privacy request, or storefront policy.

For factual corrections, editorial questions, rights inquiries, or reprint requests, email [support@outsideinprint.org](mailto:support@outsideinprint.org).

## What to include

For transaction help, include the Square order, payment, or subscription identifier and a short description of the problem.

Do not email a full card number, card-security code, password, government identity document, tax credential, or account-recovery code.

Outside In Print ordinarily responds to commerce-support messages within two business days.

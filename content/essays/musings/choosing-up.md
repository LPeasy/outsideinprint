Life presents us with an endless series of decisions to make.

Up or down?

When we take away the impressions, the basic question is always the same.

<figure class="franklin-pullquote" aria-label="Section maxim">
  <blockquote>Conserve energy or burn to grow?</blockquote>
  <figcaption>- Robby V.</figcaption>
</figure>

We can sink down, or we can let go and rise to the top.

![A mature tree grows from the same fractured stone slab, its roots following the crack and its branches spreading across a rose-gold and violet dawn sky.](oip-image:essays/choosing-up/section-1)

*Grown Through Stone.*

It might seem impossible, but the mind of man is not well understood, and certain decisions seem to have some miraculous ability to affect the physical world when carried out with unshakeable faith and relentless persistence.

Decide where you want to go ~ who you want to be.

Choose up.

The waiter passed their table with two plates of fish, each with a little pile of mango. Syd turned in his chair to see where they went.

**Syd:** We’ve been eating well while we’ve been here.

**Oliver:** I have two cheese-related questions for you.

**Syd:** I’m not a big cheese person, so I don’t know if I’ll be able to…

**Oliver:** Okay, okay, that’s fair. If you had to eliminate all the cheese except one, which cheese would you keep?

Syd looked up from his menu.

**Syd:** I have a small question. Why do I have to eliminate all the cheese?

**Oliver:** You can only keep one. The others go.

**Syd:** In my refrigerator, or everywhere?

**Oliver:** Everywhere.

**Syd:** Oh boy.

He put his menu down.

**Syd:** Do people know it was me?

**Oliver:** No.

**Syd:** All right.

Oliver waited. At the next table, a woman squeezed a lime over her fish.

**Syd:** I think cheddar. Cheddar is just so personal.

**Oliver:** Personal?

**Syd:** Yeah. It’s the one I have in the house. I’m standing in the kitchen, I cut off a piece, eat it. Cut off another piece.

**Oliver:** While you’re deciding what to have for dinner.

**Syd:** Yeah, yeah. And then I have mac and cheese. Or a grilled cheese.

**Oliver:** What about mozzarella?

**Syd:** What about it?

**Oliver:** You’re getting rid of it.

**Syd:** Oh no!

**Oliver:** Pizza.

**Syd:** I was thinking about sandwiches...

The waiter stopped beside them with her pad.

“Have you decided?”

**Syd:** Not really.

**Oliver:** Could we have another minute? Thanks.

She nodded and went to collect some empty plates.

**Syd:** Could you put cheddar on a pizza?

**Oliver:** You could, I guess.

**Syd:** Would you?

**Oliver:** I’d have to, wouldn’t I? Pizza's gotta have cheese.

Syd rubbed his chin.

**Syd:** I don’t like that answer. I don't like that at all.

**Oliver:** Parmesan?

**Syd:** Stop naming cheeses!

Oliver tore a piece from the bread in the basket.

**Syd:** What are you keeping?

**Oliver:** Also cheddar, if I have to eliminate the rest of them.

**Syd:** Then why did you bring up pizza?

**Oliver:** Because I’d miss it. Oh, how I would miss it.

**Syd:** Me too, my friend.

Oliver dipped his bread in the oil.

**Oliver:** My second question is: which cheese are you most excited to see on a menu when you’re about to order food? And it’s okay if your answer is the same.

**Syd:** Burrata! Easy.

**Oliver:** That was quick.

**Syd:** I like when they cut it open and all the stuff comes out. With the tomatoes. Some bread. What about you?

**Oliver:** Gouda. Especially smoked Gouda on a burger.

**Syd:** Oh yeah, so good.

Oliver found the burger on the menu and read the description.

**Oliver:** They have that here.

**Syd:** Well, there you go!

Oliver kept his finger beside it.

**Oliver:** You’d still save cheddar over burrata, though?

**Syd:** Well, yeah. Cheddar’s at home. I don’t go out and get excited because they’ve got some too.

**Oliver:** But you like it.

**Syd:** I like it a lot. It's probably my favorite.

Oliver laughed into his water.

**Syd:** I hardly ever have burrata. When I see it, I think, oh, that would be good. I don’t know if I’d want to eat it every day.

**Oliver:** You wouldn’t have to eat it every day.

**Syd:** No, but I’d have to eat it every time I wanted cheese. That’s a lot to ask of something I only like with tomatoes. I love cheese. This is tough.

Syd shook his head, took the last piece of bread, broke it, and put half on Oliver’s plate.

**Syd:** I don’t want to get rid of mozzarella.

**Oliver:** You can change your answer.

**Syd:** No, I’ve picked cheddar. I’m just unhappy about it. Cheese is important.

The waiter returned.

“I can take your order now.”

**Syd:** What was that fish you carried past? With the mango?

“The grilled fish special! Rice, mango, lime sauce.”

**Syd:** That sounds really good. I’ll have that.

Oliver lifted his finger from the burger.

**Oliver:** Same for me, please.

She collected the menus.

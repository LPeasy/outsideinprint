*The first four installments followed [Walt and Roy's Florida project](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/walt-disney-world-opening-1971/), [EPCOT's road to opening](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/epcot-center-opening-1982/), [the studios, nightlife, and hotels](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/disney-world-expansion-studios-pleasure-island-hotels/), and [Animal Kingdom's arrival](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/animal-kingdom-opening-1998/). With four major parks in place, Disney entered the 2000s with a resort to fill and a vacation to arrange.*

## EPCOT Welcomes 2000

Mickey's giant gloved hand held a wand above Spaceship Earth, with the year 2000 in red beside the silver sphere. Disney's [annual report pictured the greeting](https://www.annualreports.com/HostedData/AnnualReportArchive/w/NYSE_DIS_2000.pdf#page=20).

![Spaceship Earth beside a giant Mickey Mouse hand, wand, and red 2000 numerals.](oip-image:essays/disney-world-2000s/section-1)

*Spaceship Earth dresses for the new millennium. [Photograph by Gabriel Vanslette](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Epcot_1999_-_panoramio.jpg), [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).*

EPCOT began its [fifteen-month Millennium Celebration](https://www.annualreports.com/HostedData/AnnualReportArchive/w/NYSE_DIS_1999.pdf#page=18) on October 1, 1999. A parade and a new nighttime show joined the occasion. Elsewhere in the company, employees tested computer systems and prepared for the date change. Reservations and attraction controls were among the things that needed to recognize the new year. The [1999 report documented the preparations](https://www.annualreports.com/HostedData/AnnualReportArchive/w/NYSE_DIS_1999.pdf#page=52).

Micke(y) 2K.  The computer staff had contingency plans... the mouse was ready with his magic wand.

Disney introduced *FASTPASS*, which let a guest collect a return window for a participating attraction and spend the interval elsewhere. It carried no extra charge. Disney described the system in its [1999 account of the parks](https://www.annualreports.com/HostedData/AnnualReportArchive/w/NYSE_DIS_1999.pdf#page=17).

The small ticket offered something useful on a holiday ~ permission to do something besides stand in line. A family could walk away without giving up its planned ride. The clock would call them back.

## Disney World's Slowdown After the Millennium

The celebration helped make fiscal 2000 a strong year. Disney reported record attendance and occupied hotel room nights at Walt Disney World. The following year brought a harder comparison and a weaker economy. Then the September 11 attacks brought closures and travel cancellations in the final weeks of Disney's fiscal year. Its [2001 report distinguished those effects](https://www.annualreports.com/HostedData/AnnualReportArchive/w/NYSE_DIS_2001.pdf#page=58).

Walt Disney World's revenue fell by $496 million in fiscal 2002. Disney attributed the decline to weaker attendance, spending, and hotel occupancy amid disrupted tourism and economic weakness. The [filing described a business with fewer guests and softer spending](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1001039/000095014802002854/v86195e10vk.htm).

A hotel room could not be put away until travelers felt better. The buildings needed care, and the next night brought another room to sell.

Disney also eased back from the heavy building program of the preceding years. Across its parks-and-resorts division, capital spending fell from $1.278 billion in fiscal 2001 to $577 million in 2003. Those figures covered more than Florida. Management's [2003 explanation pointed to the completion of major projects](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1001039/000095014803002883/v95088e10vk.htm), while work continued on individual attractions and resort facilities.

## Mission: SPACE and Pop Century Open in 2003

At EPCOT, Mission: SPACE offered a [simulated journey to Mars](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/10/2003-Annual-Report.pdf#page=28). Disney and Hewlett-Packard marked the formal launch of Mission: SPACE on October 9, 2003, alongside a ten-year agreement between the companies. HP engineers worked with Disney's Imagineers on the attraction's technology and its post-show Advanced Training Lab. Their [joint announcement described years of collaboration](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/press-releases/hp-and-disney-launch-10-year-strategic-alliance/).

HP supplied expertise and put its name beside a visitor experience, following a pattern familiar from [EPCOT's development](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/epcot-center-opening-1982/).

Back on Earth, Disney opened Pop Century on December 14, 2003. Its 2,880 rooms belonged to the company's value category. The [2004 Fact Book recorded the date and room count](https://adisney.go.com/adownload/investorrelations/disney_fb04.pdf#page=36).

## Magic Your Way and Magical Express: Selling the Whole Vacation

On January 2, 2005, Disney introduced *Magic Your Way*. Buyers chose the length of their admission and could add [park-hopping or water-park access](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/app/uploads/ir/2006/events/jh-boa-ml-2006-0329-transcript.pdf#page=4). The daily admission cost declined on longer tickets. Disney advertised a six-night, seven-day hotel-and-ticket package for a family of four starting at $1,500. Dining was an additional choice. The [original announcement laid out the offer](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/press-releases/walt-disney-world-introduces-innovative-new-ticket-plan/).

The advertised package included the room and park tickets. Meals and other purchases added to the bill. Extending the stay could lower admission's daily price while adding hotel nights and meals.

The ticket encouraged visitors to consider the whole week. A family choosing admission also had to decide how long to stay and which extras were worth buying.

The journey from the airport became part of the offer too. Disney announced *Magical Express* for May 5, 2005. Eligible Disney hotel guests could take a complimentary coach from Orlando International Airport, with luggage delivered to the hotel. The [period announcement described transport and baggage service](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/press-releases/disney-destinations-worldwide-look-to-2005-for-a-golden-year-first-ever-global-portfolio-marketing-campaign-to-launch-new-years-day/).

![A blue, silver, and yellow Disney's Magical Express coach parked outside a resort.](oip-image:essays/disney-world-2000s/section-2)

*A Magical Express coach in May 2008: the airport transfer became part of a Disney hotel stay. [Photograph by Michael Gray](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Disney_Magical_Express_Bus_%282531699826%29.jpg), [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/).*

A suitcase could make its own way to the room. That was a respectable trick, even without a wand.

The offer depended on the booking. Swan and Dolphin guests were excluded, as were guests at Shades of Green and the Hotel Plaza Boulevard hotels. As the [third installment's hotel story showed](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/disney-world-expansion-studios-pleasure-island-hotels/), an address on Disney property did not mean Disney owned the hotel. The airport service made those business distinctions visible in a visitor's itinerary.

Disney explained its interest plainly. In March 2006, parks finance chief Jim Hunt reported company research showing that Disney hotel guests spent roughly two more days in its parks and over 60 percent more on food, beverages, and merchandise with Disney than guests staying elsewhere. His [presentation connected hotel stays to vacation spending](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/app/uploads/ir/2006/events/jh-boa-ml-2006-0329-transcript.pdf#page=5).

The figures compared groups of guests, rather than measuring what a bus ride caused them to spend. They explained the attraction of the hotel business. Disney could handle the airport trip for guests and encourage them to spend more of their vacation money at the resort.

The programs began under Michael Eisner. Bob Iger succeeded him as chief executive in [October 2005](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1001039/000119312506004951/ddef14a.htm). The handover occurred while this new way of selling the resort was already in use.

## Expedition Everest: Building a Reason to Return

Tickets and airport transport could help sell a trip... but Disney also needed something at the other end of it.

![Exposed steel framing and unfinished rockwork of Expedition Everest behind construction walls announcing a 2006 opening.](oip-image:essays/disney-world-2000s/section-3)

*Expedition Everest under construction in January 2005. Behind the "Opening 2006" signs, steel and rockwork were becoming a mountain. [Photograph by Raul654](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Expedition_Everest_under_construction.jpg), [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).*

At Animal Kingdom, that something rose into a mountain. Expedition Everest put a Himalayan adventure and the legend of the Yeti inside the Florida park.

In [January 2005](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Expedition_Everest_under_construction.jpg), work platforms wrapped around exposed steel above patches of unfinished rockwork. The mountain showed how it was made.

Disney's [December 2005 announcement](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/press-releases/ringing-in-the-next-50-years-of-disney-vacation-magic/) described Imagineers joining Discovery Networks and Conservation International on an expedition to the Himalayas as they researched the attraction. It opened in [April 2006](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/press-releases/walt-disney-parks-and-resorts-to-debut-new-attractions-and-entertainment-in-2007/).

Everest was a [roller coaster](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/app/uploads/ir/2006/events/jh-boa-ml-2006-0329-transcript.pdf#page=3) built around the story of a Yeti guarding the route to Mount Everest. A large mechanical Yeti waited for riders.

The [fourth installment](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/animal-kingdom-opening-1998/) followed the work of making a park around living animals. Everest added a creature whose feeding arrangements were considerably simpler.

## How Disney Paid for New Rides and Resorts

Disney reported that $4.3 billion in operating cash funded $1.8 billion in capital expenditure in fiscal 2005. These were company-wide totals, covering its businesses beyond the Florida resort. The [annual filing identified the cash source](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1001039/000095014805000128/v14978e10vk.htm). It did not give Everest a separate price tag.

[Hunt described a shift](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/app/uploads/ir/2006/events/jh-boa-ml-2006-0329-transcript.pdf#page=7) from the heavy construction of 1996-2001 toward maintaining the parks and making selected additions. Everest could join an operating resort, with its roads and hotels already serving visitors.

Disney also built places for returning guests to stay. Sales of vacation-ownership interests through Disney Vacation Club could return development money while construction continued. In the same [2006 presentation](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/app/uploads/ir/2006/events/jh-boa-ml-2006-0329-transcript.pdf#page=8), Hunt expected Disney to recover its investment in all three phases of Saratoga Springs before completing the first building of the third phase.

Hotels earned their room revenue one night at a time. Vacation Club buyers committed money to future stays.

## Pleasure Island Closes Its Clubs as New Villas Open

In June 2008, Disney announced that Pleasure Island's clubs would close on September 27. The company proposed more shopping and dining and attributed the change to guest requests. Several restaurants and shops would remain open during the transition. The [announcement preserved by Jeff Lange that month](https://www.jefflangedvd.com/news/archives/2008/06/entry_165.html) described the replacement plans.

The clubs supplied the night out in [A Longer Stay](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/disney-world-expansion-studios-pleasure-island-hotels/). Their regulars did not all want a different evening. A writer at Save Pleasure Island, who opposed the closing, [reported the final night the following afternoon](https://savepleasureisland.blogspot.com/2008/09/saturday-night-report.html). At Mannequins, the crowd called for one more song, and the DJ returned to play it.

Disney was changing the offer. Some of its customers wanted to keep the party going.

![Bay Lake Tower's curved exterior above its entrance drive in July 2009.](oip-image:essays/disney-world-2000s/section-4)

*Bay Lake Tower in July 2009, bringing vacation-ownership villas beside the Contemporary Resort. [Photograph by Scdis32](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:BayLakeTower.JPG), released into the public domain by the photographer.*

Bay Lake Tower, Treehouse Villas, and the second phase of Animal Kingdom Villas opened in 2009, as Disney recorded in its [annual filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1001039/000119312509245848/d10k.htm). Vacation Club's [fall magazine presented Bay Lake Tower beside the Contemporary](https://disneyvacationclub.disney.go.com/media/dvc/languagespecific/eng/member/justformembers/disneyfilesmagazine/2009/DisneyFilesMagazine_Fall2009.pdf). Disney was selling vacation-ownership interests beside a hotel that dated to the resort's opening in 1971.

The additional accommodation entered a weaker market. In fiscal 2009, Disney's East Coast hotel occupancy fell from 90 percent to 87 percent. Spending per occupied room also fell, from $223 to $205, a measure that included spending beyond the room charge. The [same filing reported the decline](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1001039/000119312509245848/d10k.htm). Construction could add rooms. Families would decide whether and when to book them.

## Disney's 2009 Plans for Fantasyland and Star Tours

In September 2009, parks chairman Jay Rasulo presented a new set of reasons to come back. Disney announced a large Fantasyland expansion, including an attraction for the Little Mermaid, more room for Dumbo, and dining inside the Beast's castle. A new version of Star Tours was planned for 2011. The [release distributed at the D23 Expo](https://archive.wdwnt.com/2009/09/star-tours-ii-fantasyland-expansion-press-release/) set a 2013 target for the Fantasyland expansion.

They were plans for the next decade. Their construction, revisions, and openings would supply the next chapter.

The 2000s began with a giant year above EPCOT and a return time on a small ticket. By the decade's end, Disney could arrange much of the journey from the airport to a mountain in Florida. Behind it stood construction bills, hotel reservations, and people deciding what a holiday was worth. Out in the park, a guest could check the ticket once more. It was nearly time to board. The mountain looked peaceful enough ~ which, in a place built by storytellers, was probably asking for trouble.

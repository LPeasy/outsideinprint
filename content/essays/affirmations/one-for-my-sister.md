The bookshop owner brings out a chair while he unpacks his books. He sets a small stack at each end of the table and lays a copy in the middle, where someone can pick it up. Beside it, he puts his pen.

<figure class="franklin-pullquote" aria-label="Affirmation">
  <blockquote>I am an amazing good luck generator.</blockquote>
  <figcaption>- Robby V.</figcaption>
</figure>

He sits with his coffee. The shop door stands open behind him. Inside, someone is sorting books onto a cart, humming along with the radio.

A woman slows beside the table. She reads the cover, then picks up the book and turns it over.

He sets his cup down and smiles at her.

*All the good things are coming to me right now.*

She opens to the first page.

He knows that page well. He can picture the sentences as her eyes move across it. Near the bottom, she smiles. A little farther down, she laughs through her nose and shakes her head.

He feels himself smiling, too.

*Something positively amazing is unfolding for me right now.*

She turns the page.

He lifts his coffee and settles back in the chair. A breeze stirs the edge of the tablecloth against his knee.

She reads with one finger tucked beneath the next page. Now and then, her thumb moves along the paper. He takes a sip. The radio changes songs.

*I get out of the way, and the blessings come pouring in.*

When she looks up, she keeps her place with her finger. She glances at his pen and asks him to sign it.

He opens to the title page. She gives him her name, spelling it while he writes.

Before he can put the pen down, she reaches for another copy.

“One for my sister.”

He opens that one, too.

She spells the second name more slowly. He checks it with her, then finishes the inscription. She reads it and rests her hand on the page for a moment, letting the ink dry.

From her purse, she takes a few folded bills and lays them beside his cup.

*Money flows easily into my life.*

He gives her change and slips a bookmark into each book. She puts her sister’s copy in her bag. Her own stays in her hand, one finger already finding the place where she stopped.

He watches her go, then takes a fresh copy from the box and sets it within reach.

## Echoes

I am an amazing good luck generator.\
All the good things are coming to me right now.\
Something positively amazing is unfolding for me right now.\
I get out of the way, and the blessings come pouring in.\
Money flows easily into my life.

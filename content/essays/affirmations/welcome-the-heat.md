The meeting ends.

I close my laptop.
Chairs scrape back, the door swings open, and the room clears around me.

The plan I worked on all week has failed.

My mind starts to reach for an explanation ~ an excuse, really ~ before I even stand up.

Maybe the timing was wrong?
Maybe I needed one more day.
Maybe I...

The dark laptop screen catches my reflection.

My jaw is tight.

My eyes are searching.

I rest both hands on the table and say ~

“I turn off the impression.”

My face relaxes. The tension releases from my body.

I open my notebook and write one honest sentence: "the plan failed."

Under it, I write ~

"I turn failure into opportunity."

I toss the laptop in my bag, tuck the notebook under my arm, and walk toward the glass door.

Bright afternoon light floods the threshold. Heat rolls across the pavement outside.

A car waits across the lot. I step out and the sun meets me, heats me.

I say ~

<figure class="franklin-pullquote" aria-label="Affirmation">
  <blockquote>I welcome the heat because I am what remains after the fire.</blockquote>
  <figcaption>- Robby V.</figcaption>
</figure>

![The same anonymous man sits on a low concrete wall outside the office, writing in an open notebook across his knees while his laptop bag rests beside him.](oip-image:essays/welcome-the-heat/section-1)

*I turn bad into good. I turn failure into opportunity.*

The heat melts my shoulders. I open my eyes and glance at the sun.
I loosen my collar and walk to the low wall beside the building.

I sit down and open the notebook across my knees.

The bad result is on the page, and so is my response.

I write ~

“I turn bad into good. I turn failure into opportunity.”

One failed afternoon is my current circumstance.
My vision reaches farther, looks better, and
seems more real to me than what just happened.

<figure class="franklin-pullquote" aria-label="Affirmation">
  <blockquote>I have more faith in my vision than in my current circumstances.</blockquote>
  <figcaption>- Robby V.</figcaption>
</figure>

Faith takes a step into the unknown.

Faith makes the call, and accepts the results.

Faith knows that every stumble, every misstep, every could've been ~
is really a lesson that I need to learn.

I welcome the heat because I am what remains after the fire.

"I welcome the heat because I am what remains after the fire."

"I turn bad into good. I turn failure into opportunity."

"I have more faith in my vision than in my current circumstances."

"I turn off the impression."

I let go and rise up.

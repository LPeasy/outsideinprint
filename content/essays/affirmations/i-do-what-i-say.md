Pay close attention to the things you say ~ you are bound to get what you ask for.

<figure class="franklin-pullquote" aria-label="Affirmation">
  <blockquote>I do the things that I say I’m going to do.</blockquote>
  <figcaption>- Robby V.</figcaption>
</figure>

Say this to yourself out loud, in a quiet place, completely alone ~

"I do what I say I do."

Say this to yourself, and PUT SOME EMOTION INTO IT ~

"I Do The Things That I Say I'm Going To Do."

I get up every morning, and I thank Heaven for my life. I pay attention to my words, and I try my best.

I keep my promises. I believe in myself. I believe that we get what we ask for in life. We receive exactly what we believe we deserve.

So, do what you say you do. Not what they do ~

As simply as possible...

<figure class="franklin-pullquote" aria-label="Affirmation">
  <blockquote>Do what say do, not what they do.</blockquote>
  <figcaption>- Robby V.</figcaption>
</figure>

*Walk your own path.*

We inevitably believe the things that we say to ourselves, about ourselves, and about the world.

THIS IS NOT THEORY. THIS IS LAW. There is no escaping the power of belief.

You can THINK you want to change all you want, but until your subconscious accepts the "truth" through REPETITION AND CONSISTENT INSTRUCTION, you'll always be "trying to change."

Tell yourself exactly who you are, convince yourself that you have arrived, and your subconscious will deliver that version into reality.

This concept is called AUTO-SUGGESTION, and it is one of the most powerful tools we have as rational creatures to control ourselves, redefine our identities, shift our realities, and change our lives.

Our words inform our identities. The way we speak about ourselves becomes who we are ~ and the coolest thing ever is that you can determine EXACTLY what and who you are going to be by COMMANDING your lizard brain and feeding it whatever you want.

This is a powerful truth. The same mechanism bites both ways ~ tell yourself you can't do something and you have no chance.

On the other hand ~ YOU CAN, IF YOU BELIEVE THAT YOU CAN.

Tell yourself you have UNSHAKEABLE FAITH and watch how your life changes.

YOU CAN CONVINCE YOURSELF OF ANYTHING. Be careful ~ and always BE KIND TO YOURSELF.

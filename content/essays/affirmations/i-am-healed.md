How do you speak to your body?

Your words carry instructions. Your thoughts direct your attention. Your attention shapes your choices, and your choices shape your life.

Speak with love. Speak with gratitude. Speak LIFE into yourself.

<figure class="franklin-pullquote" aria-label="Affirmation">
  <blockquote>I am healthy and I'm clean!</blockquote>
  <figcaption>— Robby V.</figcaption>
</figure>

Look directly into your own eyes and say it ~

“I am healthy and I'm clean!”

Stand up straight. Fill your lungs. Put some strength behind the words and really say it this time ~

“I AM HEALTHY AND I'M CLEAN!”

Feel your feet on the ground. Feel the breath moving through your body. Feel the steady rhythm of your heart.

You are alive. You are here. Life is moving through you.

Go outside and let the sun find your face. Take a long walk. Drink a tall glass of water. Prepare a beautiful meal. Open the windows and fill your home with fresh air.

Give your body everything it needs to flourish.

Health grows through simple acts repeated with love ~ nourishing food, deep sleep, joyful movement, honest conversation, laughter, sunlight, friendship, prayer, and peace.

Choose one good thing and do it today.

Then choose another.

A clean life feels bright and spacious. Your thoughts become clear. Your home becomes peaceful. Your intentions become honest. Your choices become simple.

Welcome everything that restores you.

Your body carries a quiet intelligence. Your heart keeps time. Your lungs draw in the world. Your blood moves warmth through you. Every breath is another gift of life.

Thank your body.

Speak to it as a trusted friend. Give it your attention. Give it movement and rest. Give it patience, kindness, and care.

Work with your body. Listen closely. Become its friend and faithful companion.

<figure class="franklin-pullquote" aria-label="Affirmation">
  <blockquote>I AM HEALED!</blockquote>
  <figcaption>— Robby V.</figcaption>
</figure>

![An anonymous seated person rests one hand over the heart in warm morning light beside a window.](oip-image:essays/i-am-healed/section-1)

*Thank your body.*

Say it slowly and feel the meaning of every word ~

“I. AM. HEALED.”

HEALED.

Whole. Complete. Renewed. Filled with life.

Let the word change your posture. Let it lift your head and settle your breath. Let it guide the way you walk into the day.

Move like someone who values life.

Eat like someone who values life.

Rest like someone who values life.

Speak like someone who values life.

Every loving action strengthens the person you have declared yourself to be.

One good meal.

One long walk.

One peaceful night.

One honest conversation.

One healing choice.

One grateful breath.

Each choice joins the next. Each day builds upon the last. Soon, health becomes the rhythm of your life.

Some days call for movement. Some days call for rest. Some days call for solitude, and others call for the company of a dear friend.

Listen and respond with care.

Trust the wisdom of steady progress. Give yourself the time and grace to grow. Celebrate every sign of strength, clarity, energy, and peace.

Speak in the present tense.

I AM.

“I am healthy and I'm clean.”

“I AM HEALED!”

Allow those words to settle deep within you. Carry them into your meals, your walks, your work, your home, and your relationships.

My mind is clear.

My body is strong.

My spirit is bright.

My life is clean.

My heart is grateful.

I welcome health. I welcome strength. I welcome renewal.

I AM HEALED!

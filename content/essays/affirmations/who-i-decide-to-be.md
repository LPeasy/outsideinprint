On my morning walk, I reach a turn where the path disappears behind a row of trees. I can only see a few steps ahead.

I used to slow down and study the ground. I'd wait until I felt certain about what to do next... 
Today I'm not missing a beat. I let the turn come to me.

At the bend, I trust my instincts and move boldly.

<figure class="franklin-pullquote" aria-label="Affirmation">
  <blockquote>I'm not who I used to be... I'm who I decide to be.</blockquote>
  <figcaption>- Robby V.</figcaption>
</figure>

The person I was, the decisions I made, everything that I was put me where I am today. 

I like to thank Past Me sometimes. So I thank him, learn from him, and keep on walking. My old habits don't get to choose today's direction. Today belongs to the choices I make now. I'm not who I think I am, or who you think I am, or anything like that ~

I'm who I choose to be, right now. 

I choose patience when I want to rush. 

I choose surrender when I want control.

If I think of an excuse, I stamp it out. 

I think --> I say --> I believe --> I do --> I AM. 

The path curves beneath my feet. The trees block the view for a moment, then open onto a new stretch of morning. I say the next line aloud ~

"I embrace change and lean into the bends."

I straighten up and take a deep breath. Then I stop asking the path to stay straight and accept the gentle curves and obscured vision. I lean into it and place my foot on the ground and gently push myself along.

I decide that no man will ever again hear me complaining about my life and finding fault. I realize that the Universe converts and fixes everything which stands in its way or opposes it. I welcome everything that I see and determine to use it all according to its value. 

![From high overhead, the same barefoot man floats face-up with hands behind his head while a blue-violet river bends between water lilies and dark green banks toward gold light.](oip-image:essays/who-i-decide-to-be/section-1)

*I let the river of life power me forward.*

Change once frightened me... when I was always treating it like an interruption. As if, somehow, I was meant to stand still and endure like a rock on the bottom of the riverbed, eternally resistant. 

On this walk, I understand the opposite. The flow of the river pulls me ever onward to a place that I could never have seen while lodged into the sand ~ a place of infinite beauty ~ more than I could have dreamed. 
A place of possibility and opportunity. I'm swept on and around a bend, golden light tracing my path with the current and disappearing around and around again.

I say ~

"I let the river of life power me forward."

I quit trying to force my way through. I step into the flow and LET GO. 
The current meets me where I am, and I allow myself to be swept along.

I keep my hands open. I breathe. I let all the weight I have been carrying slide off my shoulders and disappear into the depths. 

The river bends? I bend with it. 

The path ahead curves again and again and again ~

I smile and walk forward, trusting in faith.

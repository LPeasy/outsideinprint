Some mornings, I picture a river at first light.

The bank behind me sits in deep blue shadow. Ahead, a line of stones crosses
the water toward the sun. I can see the next stone clearly. After that, the
path bends into mist. I see myself striking out boldly and trusting to Faith.

Part of me wants the whole crossing revealed before I take that first step. I want to
know where every stone rests, which ones wobble, and exactly where I will land.
I want motion without uncertainty. I want to know...

That's when I tell myself ~

<figure class="franklin-pullquote" aria-label="Affirmation">
  <blockquote>I am not afraid. Fear is a lie.</blockquote>
  <figcaption>- Robby V.</figcaption>
</figure>

I don't use these words to pretend like I don't feel anything. I use them to remember who decides what happens next. I am the only person who has authority over me.

Fear has no power over me.

I am courageous and strong!

My courage does not need a grand entrance. It can be quiet and exact. I make the call. 
I begin the work. I say what I mean. I place one foot on the next stone 
and the next and the next ~
and I keep going no matter what.

Strength doesn't mean clinging to my first idea after I see a better way. I don't 
prove my resolve by marching in the wrong direction. 

I prove it by being present HERE and NOW. 

I look. I listen. I learn. I keep moving.

I do not quit. 

<figure class="franklin-pullquote" aria-label="Affirmation">
  <blockquote>I act now. I do not delay. I trust my instincts.</blockquote>
  <figcaption>- Robby V.</figcaption>
</figure>

Instinct tells me when to act. Attention keeps me balanced and ready.

The next stone may sit farther to the left than I expected. The one beyond it
may ask for a shorter step. I adjust my footing and keep on keepin' not stoppin'.

I move boldly, and I correct as I go.

![A bird's-eye view shows an anonymous figure in a rolled-sleeve gray shirt and brown trousers crossing stepping stones through misty blue-green water toward golden light.](oip-image:essays/correct-as-i-go/section-1)

*I move boldly, and I correct as I go.*

Bold movement and active adjustment belong together in my life. 
One gives me motion. The other keeps me headed in the right direction. 
I can commit without pretending to know everything. 
I can change course without abandoning myself.

I can't see all the way across, so I keep my head up and my eyes open. 
I'm always ready for the bends, and I embrace them when they come.
And you can bet that they will come ~

This is how I cross the water.

I choose a direction. I take the best step forward I can. 
I feel the stone beneath my foot. I raise my eyes and find the next one.

I don't wait on the other bank for perfect certainty. I don't mistake delay for
wisdom. I don't wait for the fog to clear.

I don't need to see all the way across the river! 

I trust myself enough to start walking, 
and I respect myself enough to adjust as I go.

The whole path does not need to appear at once.

The next stone is waiting in the light.

Step out and have FAITH!

The last shirt lands on the couch. A sock tumbles out of its sleeve.

He picks it up and looks for its match. There it is, hiding behind the laundry basket.

“Thought you could get away, did you?”

He folds the pair together and drops it into the drawer.

The apartment is quiet except for the ceiling fan and the faint sound of traffic outside. The afternoon sun falls across the living room floor.

He stretches his arms over his head, then lets them fall to his sides. His feet are bare against the cool floor.

He takes a long, easy breath.

*I am at peace. I’m safe in my own body.*

There is nothing he needs to do this minute. The laundry can wait. So can the dishes.

He sits down on the couch and looks around.

The room is familiar. His books are on the shelf. His shoes are by the door. A glass of water sits on the little table beside his chair.

He knows where everything is.

*My mind is clear and my heart is strong.*

A melody comes into his head.

He hums a few notes, then repeats them. They sound good together. He gives them a little rhythm, tapping his fingers against his knee.

Soon he's singing.

“My mind is clear, and my heart is strong…”

He tries another line. It doesn't quite fit. He laughs and tries again.

There it is!

He stands up and sings the little tune again, louder this time. He has no idea where it came from, but he likes it.

He makes up another verse.

The words are silly. Some of them don't even rhyme. He sings them anyway, taking a little turn across the living room floor.

One of the floorboards creaks beneath his foot.

For a moment, he stops, almost as if someone might have caught him.

Then he laughs.

Caught him doing what?

Having a good time?

He starts again.

*I don’t feel anxiety. What I feel is readiness.*

He doesn't need to know the next line before he sings it. He can make it up when he gets there.

He doesn't need an audience, either.

Nobody is watching. Nobody is waiting for him to do something impressive. He isn't trying to be anything but himself.

And he likes himself just fine.

He catches his reflection in the dark television screen.

A grown man, barefoot in his living room, singing a song he just invented.

He grins.

“Look at you!”

He takes another little turn, then drops back onto the couch.

The laundry basket is still there. The sun has moved a little farther across the floor. Outside, a car door closes, and someone calls to a friend.

He looks around the room again.

What a nice place to be.

*I deeply appreciate where I am.*

He picks up the next shirt and begins folding it.

The melody is still in his head.

He sings it softly as he works.

And when he reaches the part he likes best, he sings it loud.

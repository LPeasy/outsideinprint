My wallet is closed on the kitchen counter.

Beside it sits a cup of coffee, my keys, and a note from someone I love. 

Morning light fills the room. I take a deep breath. 
I relax, and I know everything is going to work out for me ~ exactly how it is supposed to. 

<figure class="franklin-pullquote" aria-label="Affirmation">
  <blockquote>I am wealthy in every sense of the word ~ more than money, gold, and material.</blockquote>
  <figcaption>- Robby V.</figcaption>
</figure>

Think about all of the people in your life who love you and want to see you happy and rich. Picture them all cheering you on. Imagine them all around you, overflowing with love and joy. 

Then say this out loud to yourself ~

“I am wealthy in every sense of the word ~ more than money, gold, and material.”

Take a deep breath in, hold it, and when you let it out say ~

“I AM WEALTHY IN EVERY SENSE OF THE WORD ~ MORE THAN MONEY, GOLD, AND MATERIAL.”

Money is part of wealth. Of course it is!
I welcome money gladly. I like what it lets me build, protect, enjoy, and give. Money flows into my life, and I share it in a spirit of generosity and gratitude.

It is okay for me to be rich. I'm allowed to be wealthy. 

In fact, I was born to be rich. 

The funny thing is, if you don't think about it too much anyways, being rich has very little to do with how much money one has.

Money is a good thing. 
Worrying about money? That is NOT a good thing. 

A wise man once said, "Having money isn't everything, but not having it is." 

Say this to yourself and feel it in your heart ~

“I'm rich in spirit and rich in love.”

Say it until you believe it.

I am sooooo RICH in SPIRIT.

I am sooooo RICH in LOVE.

I have everything I need, and I get everything I want. 
This is my life, and I am so grateful. 

I can be grateful. I will receive more and more and more!
“Thank you” and “yes, please” fit together quite nicely. 

The universe delivers exactly what we believe we deserve. To those who much is given, EVEN MORE WILL BE GIVEN. If you have, you get. If you think you cannot possibly receive, how could you?

Try this one on for size ~

“I was born to be rich.”

Throw both of your hands up to the sky and say ~

“I was born to be rich!!!”

My inheritance is happiness.
Creation is my birthright.
Help comes to me exactly when I need it, right on time, every time. 
The universe is my friend, and it always has my back. 

Remember how powerful you are. Tell yourself this ~

“I make money in my sleep! I get paid to exist.”

This might be hard to believe at first. But the truth is... ~

“I make money in my sleep! I get paid to exist.”

I make myself. I am what I say I am. 
I'm worthy. I'm valuable. I'm an infinite, powerful creator. 

My attention ~

My voice ~

My work ~

My presence ~

More than money, more than gold, more than material... I am wealthy and grateful for it. 
I was born to be rich.

<figure class="franklin-pullquote" aria-label="Affirmation">
  <blockquote>Money flows easily into my life. The Universe delivers the goods.</blockquote>
  <figcaption>- Robby V.</figcaption>
</figure>

Read that again. Command yourself ~

“Money flows easily into my life. The Universe delivers the goods.”

Now let the neighborhood hear you this time ~

“THE UNIVERSE DELIVERS THE GOODS!”

Easily doesn't mean I sit around with my eyes closed. There is no such thing as something for nothing. 

I do the work. I dig the ditch. I have unshakeable faith. Then I pay attention as the rain starts pouring in. 

A chance.
An opportunity reveals itself.
I catch an idea like a butterfly in a net. 

![A blue butterfly rests on a blank open notebook beside a pen in bright window sunlight.](/images/essays/rich-in-spirit/section-1.png)

*An answer arrives.*

The answer to a hard question becomes obvious.
A stranger offers me some strange, unexpected boon at no cost to me.
A call from a friend arrives out of the blue ~ right when I need it.

Money flows into my life. 
Welcome it.

I am offered help without having to ask. 
Accept it.

Inspiration delivers an idea like a shooting star. 
Create it.

I feel surrounded by love from everyone around me.
I let the love in.

I do not stand at the door arguing with Life.
I say thank you, and open my arms wide in total acceptance.

Morning light fills the room.

My life is full of wealth. I am rich ~ so rich. 

“I'm rich in spirit and rich in love.”

“I was born to be rich.”

“I make money in my sleep! I get paid to exist.”

“Money flows easily into my life. The Universe delivers the goods.”

“I am wealthy in every sense of the word ~ more than money, gold, and material.”

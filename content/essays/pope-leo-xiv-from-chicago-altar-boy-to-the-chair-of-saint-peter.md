### **Early Life and Family Roots**

![1950s Chicago](oip-image:medium/25c92cdac108c6b8135a1ab1b65c63a3b90dc9ce3ece1bfd6790741762fdbc0d)

*1950s Chicago | Source: Wikimedia Commons*


> **Before Pope Leo XIV, he was just Rob Prevost, a kid from the South
> Side of Chicago playing priest in the living
> room.**

> **Pope Leo XIV was born Robert Francis Prevost on [September 14, 1955](https://www.vatican.va/content/leo-xiv/en/biography/documents/biografia_leone-xiv.html),
> in Chicago, Illinois.**

He grew up in a devout Catholic household in Dolton, a working-class
suburb south of the city.

His parents, [**Louis and Mildred Prevost**](https://www.vatican.va/content/leo-xiv/en/biography/documents/biografia_leone-xiv.html), were both educators deeply
involved in their local parish.

- Louis, a [World War II Navy veteran and school
  superintendent](https://time.com/7298083/pope-leo-xiv-robert-prevost-american/), passed down a legacy of discipline and public
  service.
- Mildred, a [school librarian](https://time.com/7298083/pope-leo-xiv-robert-prevost-american/),
  helped shape her son's deep spirituality through regular Mass
  attendance and community involvement.

Rob was the youngest of three brothers and is said to have taken to
religion early. [**He served as an altar boy, sang in the choir, and even
play-acted Masses at home.**](https://time.com/7298083/pope-leo-xiv-robert-prevost-american/)

Family members have recalled a childhood fascination with the
priesthood, joking that he always wanted to ["play church."](https://abc7.com/post/pope-leos-brother-says-leo-xiv-told-he-would-first-american/16360332/)

> [According to his brother John, a neighbor once told him he would
> become the first American pope.](https://abc7.com/post/pope-leos-brother-says-leo-xiv-told-he-would-first-american/16360332/)

> Little did anyone know…

### **Education and Religious Formation**

> Prevost entered a minor seminary in Michigan.

> and he remained committed to the priesthood throughout his youth.

> **He graduated from [Villanova University in 1977](https://www.villanova.edu/university/media/press-releases/2025/pope.html) with a mathematics
> degree, an uncommon path for a future pope.**

**That same year, he began his novitiate with the Augustinian Order.**

He pursued theological studies in Chicago, **then continued advanced
canon-law studies in Rome.**

> He received both a **Licentiate and a Doctorate in Canon Law.**

[**He was ordained a priest in 1982 in Rome.**](https://www.vatican.va/content/leo-xiv/en/biography/documents/biografia_leone-xiv.html)

By the late 1980s, he developed a reputation for his intellect,
humility, and multilingual fluency.

### **Missionary Work in Peru**

![Prevost in 2012](/images/medium/pope-leo-xiv-from-chicago-altar-boy-to-the-chair-of-saint-peter/a4f857d3109b3e5bcbb624c04617ff0b5f05bd66cbf3d854efc69bbd6c979231.jpeg)

*Prevost in 2012 | Source: Wikimedia Commons*


> [**Fr. Prevost began missionary service in northern Peru in 1985,
> returned to the United States in 1987, and went to Trujillo in 1988.**](https://www.vatican.va/content/leo-xiv/en/biography/documents/biografia_leone-xiv.html)

**He became a beloved pastor**, seminary rector, professor, and canon
lawyer **in the Archdiocese of Trujillo.**

> **He led with quiet discipline.**

**This chapter of his life left a permanent mark.**

Prevost became **fluent in Spanish** and immersed in Peruvian life,
laying the foundation for his later **appointment as the Bishop of
Chiclayo.**

> [He became a naturalized Peruvian citizen in 2015.](https://www.vatican.va/content/leo-xiv/en/biography/documents/biografia_leone-xiv.html)

### **Global Leadership in the Augustinian Order**

![Pope Leo’s Inauguration](/images/medium/pope-leo-xiv-from-chicago-altar-boy-to-the-chair-of-saint-peter/54f9bf2dba2609d3ac58119fd2080f2c79adcb50b24b92f488cf09254d5accfa.jpeg)

*Pope Leo’s Inauguration | Source: Wikimedia Commons*


> [In 1998, he was elected Provincial Prior of the Augustinians in
> Chicago, taking office in 1999.](https://www.vatican.va/content/leo-xiv/en/biography/documents/biografia_leone-xiv.html)

**In 2001,** his global peers chose him as [**Prior General of
the worldwide Augustinian Order**](https://www.augustinianorder.org/pope-leo-xiv-son-of-saint-augustine), a role he held for 12 years.

> **As leader of the Augustinians, he visited friars around the world
> and honed a collaborative leadership style.**

After finishing his term in 2013, he returned to seminary formation work
in Chicago.

> He expected a quieter chapter ahead.

### **Return to Peru and Rise in the Vatican**

![Catedral de Chiclayo](/images/medium/pope-leo-xiv-from-chicago-altar-boy-to-the-chair-of-saint-peter/1cce7c803071816bf647efbbdd6cfb85cb0919e17b4f97a24f0184cb34d036b8.jpeg)

*Catedral de Chiclayo | Source: Wikimedia Commons*


> Pope Francis had other plans.

**In 2014,** the Pope appointed Prevost as **Apostolic Administrator of
Chiclayo, Peru,** ***later naming him bishop.***

> **His episcopal motto ~ 'In the One, we are one' ~ reflected his
> emphasis on unity and synodality (walking together in the faith).**

**He was a leader** in Latin American circles and served as **vice
president of the Peruvian Bishops' Conference.**

[**In 2023, he was called to Rome to lead the Vatican Dicastery for
Bishops,**](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/pope/news/2025-05/biography-of-robert-francis-prevost-pope-leo-xiv.html) one of the Church's most influential offices.

> [He was made a cardinal later that year.](https://www.vatican.va/content/leo-xiv/en/biography/documents/biografia_leone-xiv.html)

**As Prefect, he oversaw the appointment of bishops worldwide and
participated in high-level reforms** aligned with Pope Francis's
priorities.

### **Papal Election and Inauguration**

![Vatican City](/images/medium/pope-leo-xiv-from-chicago-altar-boy-to-the-chair-of-saint-peter/a36be69de1178f793b3ef5f45de7d901894c3afcfb1d7313ae125c8629736f57.jpg)

*Vatican City | Source: [Karim Ben Van](https://unsplash.com/@kbendakhlia)*


> Pope Francis passed away in April 2025.

During the conclave that followed, **Prevost was not widely seen as a
frontrunner**, largely because he was American.

[**But after four rounds of voting, on May 8, 2025, he was elected the
267th pope, choosing the name Leo XIV.**](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/pope/news/2025-05/pope-leo-xiv-homily-faith-college-cardinals-sistine-chapel.html)

> **He appeared in a white cassock and red mozzetta and led the crowd
> in prayer.**

> He became the first American, the first Augustinian, and the second
> pope from the Americas.

[**In his first blessing**](https://www.vatican.va/content/leo-xiv/en/messages/urbi/documents/20250508-prima-benedizione-urbietorbi.html) from the balcony of St. Peter's Basilica,

**he greeted the world simply:**

> **"Peace be with you all!"**

service in Chiclayo, Vatican service beginning in 2023, and election as
Pope Leo XIV on May 8, 2025.

### **Early Papacy: Climate, Peace, and Public Presence**

**Since taking office,**

> Pope Leo XIV has emphasized two key themes:

1.  **Climate action**
2.  **Peacemaking**

[**He introduced a new Mass formulary**](https://www.christianunity.va/content/unitacristiani/en/news/2025/2025-07-09-mass-for-the-care-of-creation.html) focused on **environmental
care** and celebrated a public **"Mass for Creation"** while on retreat
at Castel Gandolfo.

> [**"In a burning world, be it because of global warming or armed
> conflicts…"**](https://www.usccb.org/news/2025/pope-prays-conversion-those-resisting-climate-action-new-mass)

In homilies, **he has warned bluntly about climate inaction**

> referring to ["a burning world."](https://www.usccb.org/news/2025/pope-prays-conversion-those-resisting-climate-action-new-mass)

**On diplomacy,** [**he offered the Vatican as a host for peace
negotiations in Ukraine.**](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/pope/news/2025-07/pope-leo-xiv-meeting-president-ukraine-zelensky-peace-talks.html)

He has also **revived public traditions** like papal retreats and
informal catechesis, **using them as platforms for education**,
ecological dialogue, and moral leadership.

### **Character and Style**

> Pope Leo XIV is described as gentle, steady, and principled.

He is fluent in multiple languages and comfortable among both heads of
state and the poor.

As a former missionary, he prioritizes proximity over prestige.

> He's also known for having trademark "humble humor."

> Even in high office,

> his friends call him "**Padre Bob."**

### **What Comes Next**

> Leo XIV has signaled that his papacy will be both pastoral and
> reform-minded.

He supports greater **lay participation** and hinted at [**exploring digital
ethics**](https://www.vatican.va/content/leo-xiv/en/speeches/2025/may/documents/20250510-collegio-cardinalizio.html) in the future.

At the start of his tenure, his approach was clear:

> **Bridge divides, walk humbly, act boldly.**

His **background in both law and science,** paired with years of
pastoral experience, suggests he may be **uniquely positioned to tackle
complex issues** like artificial intelligence, ethics, and Church
transparency.

**Whether addressing the global climate crisis or quietly consoling
prisoners,**

> Pope Leo XIV appears intent on using his office:

> Not for grandeur, but for service.

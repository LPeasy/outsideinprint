*The first five installments followed Disney World from [Walt and Roy's Florida project](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/walt-disney-world-opening-1971/) to a resort with four major parks. [The Whole Vacation](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/disney-world-2000s/) ended with Disney's 2009 plans for Fantasyland and Star Tours. In the next decade, those plans became places to visit, while Disney changed how guests arranged the trip.*

## Star Tours Returns and New Fantasyland Opens

Steel track curves between rock faces and beneath a timber-style bridge. Water spills beside it. In an April 2014 photograph of Seven Dwarfs Mine Train, a fairy-tale landscape surrounds the machinery that will carry people through it.

![Seven Dwarfs Mine Train track curves beneath a timber-style bridge beside rockwork and a waterfall.](oip-image:essays/disney-world-2010s/section-1)

*Seven Dwarfs Mine Train in April 2014, before its May public opening. Photograph by [Josh Hallett](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Seven_Dwarfs_Mine_Train.jpg), [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/).*

The work announced at the end of the 2000s opened in stages. At Disney's Hollywood Studios, the revised Star Tours began carrying guests on May 20, 2011. George Lucas joined Disney chief executive Bob Iger for the opening. Disney's Imagineers worked with Lucasfilm and Industrial Light & Magic on the [new version of the attraction](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/star-wars-creator-george-lucas-and-disneys-bob-iger-launch-star-tours-attraction-at-walt-disney-world-resort-122324123.html).

At Magic Kingdom, New Fantasyland's December 2012 opening brought guests into the Little Mermaid's world and the Beast's castle. Be Our Guest Restaurant made the castle a place to eat as well as admire. Disney's [account of the expansion](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/experiences-innovative-ideas-bring-new-fantasyland-life-walt-disney-world-resort/) outlined the remaining additions.

Seven Dwarfs Mine Train [opened on May 28, 2014](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/seven-dwarfs-mine-train-officially-opens-to-magic-kingdom-guests/), completing the expansion. Its cars could sway as they followed the track. Snow White's companions supplied the setting for a family coaster.

Accommodation grew alongside the attractions. Art of Animation began receiving guests on May 31, 2012, with its *Finding Nemo* section. The [opening announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/disneys-art-of-animation-resort-opens-immerses-guests-in-the-middle-of-the-story-with-enchanting-tales-155917595.html) described family suites for up to six people, with separate sleeping areas and two bathrooms. Other sections would draw on *Cars*, *The Lion King*, and *The Little Mermaid*.

For a family considering the room, the second bathroom could be as persuasive as the fish.

## MagicBands and FastPass+: Planning Before Arrival

The [paper FASTPASS of the previous decade](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/disney-world-2000s/) gave a guest a return time after reaching the park. With *FastPass+*, selected attraction times could be arranged before the trip. A linked *MagicBand* could serve as park admission, open a participating Disney hotel room, and authorize purchases charged to an eligible hotel account. Disney's [January 2013 guide](https://media.disneywebcontent.com/StaticFiles/DTA-Domestic/pdf/WDW/MM_WDW_Ref_Guide_012013.pdf) outlined the system before its wider rollout.

![A pink MagicBand worn on a wrist, with a patterned bag in the background.](oip-image:essays/disney-world-2010s/section-2)

*A MagicBand in January 2014. Photograph by [Julie Friend](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Disney%27s_MagicBand.jpg), [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).*

Disney called the broader program *MyMagic+*. The *My Disney Experience* website and app helped organize the visit. FastPass+ handled participating attraction reservations. The band connected the person wearing it with the tickets and arrangements linked to an account.

By Disney's [May 2014 earnings call](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/app/uploads/ir/2014/q2/q2-fy14-earnings-transcript.pdf#page=5), management reported that MyMagic+ was available to all Walt Disney World guests, extending beyond its earlier testing.

An attraction time could be part of the itinerary before the suitcase came out. A family could leave home with some of its priorities settled, then fit meals, travel between parks, and other attractions around them.

Disney explained its interest to investors. In his [May 2014 presentation](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/app/uploads/ir/2014/events/jar-moffnath-2014-0514.pdf#page=11), finance chief Jay Rasulo described advance planning as the program's principal expected business benefit. The company could introduce more of its activities while people were arranging their Orlando trip. It also expected quicker transactions to leave guests more time for the resort.

Disney's [2014 financial filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1001039/000100103914000228/fy2014_q4x10k.htm) recorded development and rollout expenses, operating costs, and depreciation as the equipment entered use. It did not supply a separate total project cost for MyMagic+.

The company's businesses generated $9.780 billion in operating cash that fiscal year. Domestic parks-and-resorts capital spending totaled $1.184 billion, covering more than Florida or the new technology.

## Disney Springs: Shops, Restaurants, Roads and Parking

Pleasure Island supplied the night out in [A Longer Stay](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/disney-world-expansion-studios-pleasure-island-hotels/). Its clubs closed in 2008, leaving Disney to work out the next use for that part of the waterfront. In March 2013, the company [announced Disney Springs](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/announcing-disney-springs-at-walt-disney-world-resort/), a redevelopment of the wider Downtown Disney shopping, dining, and entertainment district.

The Disney Springs name became official on [September 29, 2015](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/downtown-disney-officially-becomes-disney-springs-with-enticing-new-dining-and-exciting-retail-experiences/). Town Center followed in 2016. Its designers imagined a Florida settlement growing around a spring, with buildings that appeared to acquire new uses over time. An icehouse or bottling works could become part of a restaurant's supposed past. Imagineer Theron Skees explained [the invented history behind the design](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/disney-springs-provides-an-innovative-and-immersive-experience-for-guests/).

The old town was new construction. Its weathered surfaces and changing rooflines gave shoppers something to explore between purchases. Beyond the storefronts, the development needed access for people arriving by car.

![Exposed framing and construction materials stand beside the water at the future Blaze Pizza in Disney Springs.](oip-image:essays/disney-world-2010s/section-3)

*Blaze Pizza under construction at Disney Springs in May 2016. Photograph by [elisfkc](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Blaze_Pizza_at_Disney_Springs_%2827008959242%29.jpg), [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/).*

Roads and parking involved the Reedy Creek Improvement District, the local government established during the [original Florida development](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/walt-disney-world-opening-1971/). Its 2016 bond statement recorded a $344.96 million issue in 2013 for road and parking work. The new 2016 issue supplied $165.5 million in bond principal for districtwide transportation, additional corridor and parking improvements, and district facilities. The [offering statement](https://www.oversightdistrict.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/2016A-OS_stickered.pdf#page=11) distinguished these public works from the private businesses they served.

Investors supplied money by buying the bonds. District property taxes backed repayment. Disney affiliates accounted for approximately 85 percent of assessed property value in the district, excluding their property leased to others. J.P. Morgan, BofA Merrill Lynch, Loop Capital Markets, and Morgan Stanley underwrote the [2016 issue](https://www.oversightdistrict.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/2016A-OS_stickered.pdf#page=2), helping sell it to investors.

The bond total was not a price for all of Disney Springs. Shops and restaurants required their own buildings and business arrangements, while the district borrowed for the roads and facilities around them.

## Frozen Ever After at EPCOT and Pandora at Animal Kingdom

At EPCOT, a boat ride through Norway changed its destination. Frozen Ever After [replaced Maelstrom](https://d23.com/news-briefs-june-09-2015/) and opened in June 2016, bringing Anna, Elsa, and their companions into the Norway pavilion. Imagineer Michel den Dulk described translating the film's characters and settings into the attraction in a [period interview](https://d23.com/the-epcot-insiders-guide-to-frozen-ever-after/). Elsewhere in the park that month, [Soarin' Around the World](https://d23.com/3-disney-easter-eggs-in-soarin-around-the-world/) widened its simulated flight beyond California.

Animal Kingdom was preparing an entire land around a film. In September 2011, Disney, James Cameron's Lightstorm Entertainment, and Fox announced an [agreement for Avatar attractions](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/press-releases/disney-fox-and-james-cameron-to-bring-avatar-to-life-at-disney-parks/). Disney obtained exclusive global theme-park rights, with Cameron's team participating in design and development. This arrangement began years before Disney's [2019 acquisition of much of Fox](https://investors.thewaltdisneycompany.com/files/doc_financials/2019/ar/2019-Annual-Report-1.pdf#page=33).

The choice connected a film's imaginary ecosystem with a park built around animals and their environments. [A Living Kingdom](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/animal-kingdom-opening-1998/) followed the work behind Animal Kingdom's living landscape.

By the [May 2017 dedication](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/pandora-world-avatar-dedicated-disneys-animal-kingdom/), rock formations appeared to float above the paths. Invented plants filled the ground beneath them. Disney's Imagineers worked with Lightstorm to turn images from a screen into a place with paths, buildings, and attractions.

![Invented alien plants and moss-covered rockwork in Pandora at Disney's Animal Kingdom.](oip-image:essays/disney-world-2010s/section-4)

*Pandora's invented plant life, photographed in May 2017 before the land's public opening. Photograph by [Theme Park Tourist](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pandora_%2834627168586%29.jpg), [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/).*

Pandora [opened to the public on May 27, 2017](https://d23.com/19-disney-fied-moments-of-2017/). [Flight of Passage](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/pandora-world-avatar-open-may-27-star-wars-lands-coming-2019/) offered a simulated ride on a flying banshee. Na'vi River Journey carried passengers through a glowing forest.

As early as December 2013, Rasulo [explained](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/app/uploads/ir/2013/events/jar-ubs-2013-1210.pdf#page=3) that the investment reached beyond the Avatar land. Disney was developing entertainment and other facilities to make the park a full-day destination.

By Pandora's dedication, Disney described sunset safaris, the Rivers of Light show, and projected imagery on the Tree of Life among the [park's after-dark offerings](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/pandora-world-avatar-dedicated-disneys-animal-kingdom/). Visitors had more reasons to remain into the evening.

## Disney World's Ticket Prices Follow the Calendar

The date of a visit became part of the ticket purchase too. In February 2016, Disney introduced seasonal pricing for one-day admission, assigning dates to value, regular, and peak categories. A [contemporary answer on Disney's guest-advice site](https://plandisney.disney.go.com/question/peak-dates-theme-parks-304639/) directed buyers to the calendar for the applicable dates.

By October 2018, buyers of multiday tickets were also choosing a start date. Disney's online calendar displayed prices for the selected ticket length. [Guidance published that month](https://plandisney.disney.go.com/question/trying-find-calendar-dates-show-various-prices-tickets-384658/) explained how to compare dates before purchase.

The earlier Magic Your Way ticket asked how many days a visitor wanted and which options to add. The new purchase also asked when the trip would begin. A family able to move its holiday could compare dates. A family tied to a school break would make the choice within a narrower range.

Iger set out the business reasoning in [February 2018](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/app/uploads/rai-ms-2018-0226-transcript-1.pdf#page=16). New attractions and familiar characters strengthened Disney's ability to charge for the visit. Different prices could also help spread demand across the calendar. He described smaller price increases during less busy periods, not a promise that every off-peak ticket would become cheaper.

## New Hotels and the Disney Skyliner Open in 2019

Gran Destino Tower opened at Coronado Springs in July 2019, adding accommodation to the convention resort. A [first-day account by an invited visitor](https://www.themainstreetmouse.com/2019/07/09/see-the-new-gran-destino-tower-at-coronado-springs/) recorded its July 9 opening. Riviera Resort [opened on December 16](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/disneys-riviera-resort-welcomes-guests-with-the-sights-sounds-and-flavors-of-europe-300975283.html). Disney Vacation Club sold vacation-ownership interests there.

Riviera joined the new Skyliner gondola network. The system linked selected hotels with EPCOT and Hollywood Studios, with a shared station for Pop Century and Art of Animation. Disney's [September 2019 introduction](https://d23.com/everything-you-need-to-know-about-disney-skyliner/) explained the routes and announced the September 29 start. For those guests, reaching a park could begin with a ride above the resort.

![The completed waterfront Skyliner station shared by Pop Century and Art of Animation, with gondola cabins alongside its platforms.](oip-image:essays/disney-world-2010s/section-6)

*The Skyliner station shared by Pop Century and Art of Animation, October 14, 2019. Photograph by [elisfkc](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Disney_Skyliner_Pop-Art_Station_%2848901070702%29.jpg), [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/).*

[The EPCOT Project](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/epcot-center-opening-1982/) followed Walt's proposed connections between homes, work, and a city center. Here the passengers were vacationers traveling between hotels and parks. Transport again shaped how people could use the property.

## Toy Story Land and Galaxy's Edge: Rebuilding Hollywood Studios

Disney's [studio park opened in 1989](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/disney-world-expansion-studios-pleasure-island-hotels/) with working production facilities, demonstrations of filmmaking, and The Great Movie Ride's recreated movie scenes.

Disney and Lucasfilm worked together on Star Tours before Disney [bought Lucasfilm on December 21, 2012](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/app/uploads/2015/10/2013-Annual-Report.pdf#page=87). Disney paid $2.2 billion in cash and issued 37.1 million Disney shares, valuing the purchase at about $4.1 billion. The deal included the Star Wars franchise, visual-effects company Industrial Light & Magic, and Skywalker Sound. During the October announcement, finance chief Jay Rasulo told investors that the valuation included [opportunities in parks, games, and television](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/app/uploads/ir/2012/events/lucasfilm-2012-1030-transcript.pdf#page=5). Iger also described [room to expand Star Wars in the parks](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/app/uploads/ir/2012/events/lucasfilm-2012-1030-transcript.pdf#page=10).

![Bob Iger and George Lucas stand near the center of the Star Tours opening stage, surrounded by costumed Star Wars characters and an audience.](oip-image:essays/disney-world-2010s/section-7)

*Bob Iger and George Lucas at the Star Tours opening in Disney's Hollywood Studios, May 20, 2011, before Disney's purchase of Lucasfilm. Photograph: [Star Tours Grand Opening Ceremony](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Star_Tours_Grand_Opening_Ceremony_%285741572832%29.jpg) by littlelostrobot, [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/).*

The building program announced at the [2015 D23 Expo](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/disney-news-this-week-walt-disney-parks-and-resorts-announces-new-lands-and-enhancements-the-walt-disney-studios-reveals-details-about-its-upcoming-slate-and-pixar-and-walt-disney-animation-studios/) would give guests more room inside the films' imaginary worlds.

Toy Story Land opened on June 30, 2018. Its oversized toys placed visitors in Andy's backyard, with Slinky Dog Dash and Alien Swirling Saucers joining Toy Story Mania. At the [dedication the previous day](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/toy-story-land-dedicated-at-disneys-hollywood-studios/), parks chairman Bob Chapek described Hollywood Studios' movement from showing people behind the scenes to placing them in the action.

The next summer brought a different setting. Florida's Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge opened on August 29, 2019. Visitors entered Black Spire Outpost on the invented planet Batuu. They could take a position in the Millennium Falcon's cockpit, explore shops, and buy food within the setting. Disney's [opening account](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/star-wars-galaxys-edge-opens-at-walt-disney-world-resort/) described a land built to make those ordinary holiday activities part of a Star Wars visit.

![Visitors climb broad steps among themed buildings and rocky spires at Galaxy's Edge in Disney's Hollywood Studios.](oip-image:essays/disney-world-2010s/section-5)

*Visitors walk through Galaxy's Edge at Disney's Hollywood Studios on August 29, 2019. Photograph by [Jedi94 at English Wikipedia](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Star_Wars,_Galaxy%27s_Edge,_Walt_Disney_World.jpg), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).*

Disney's [2019 annual report](https://investors.thewaltdisneycompany.com/files/doc_financials/2019/ar/2019-Annual-Report-1.pdf#page=57) recorded $3.294 billion in domestic capital expenditure within Parks, Experiences and Products. That total extended beyond Florida. The company attributed the increase over 2018 to new Walt Disney World attractions, partly offset by lower spending at Disneyland. It did not identify that annual sum as the cost of Florida's Star Wars land.

Domestic park attendance for the fiscal year was [comparable to the previous year](https://investors.thewaltdisneycompany.com/files/doc_financials/2019/ar/2019-Annual-Report-1.pdf#page=42). In November, finance chief Christine McCarthy [reported](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/app/uploads/2019/11/q4-fy19-earnings-transcript.pdf#page=8) that higher Florida attendance, guest spending, and occupied room nights helped offset Galaxy's Edge operating costs in the final quarter. Hurricane Dorian also affected the period.

Disney was already describing more work for EPCOT. Its [August 2019 announcements](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/disney-parks-experiences-and-products-reveals-next-generation-of-immersive-storytelling-in-disney-parks/) included Guardians of the Galaxy, Remy, and Moana experiences, along with a new arrangement of the park's neighborhoods. Those were proposals and projects in development as the decade ended.

Galaxy's Edge also opened in stages. Rise of the Resistance began receiving guests on [December 5, 2019](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/star-wars-rise-of-the-resistance-opens-at-walt-disney-world-resort/). Imagineering and Lucasfilm combined several ride systems to carry visitors through capture and escape aboard a First Order Star Destroyer. Trackless vehicles appeared to be driven by droids. The designers could decide where the journey went without laying a visible rail along the floor.

For now, a visitor could arrange the date, reserve an attraction, choose a room, and ride from a hotel toward the parks. Disney supplied more ways to plan the journey and more places for it to go. Inside Rise of the Resistance, the floor offered no track to follow. A droid sat at the controls, and the way out lay somewhere ahead. After all that planning, there was something to be said for letting the little fellow drive.

*This concluding installment follows the Florida project from [Walt and Roy's plans](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/walt-disney-world-opening-1971/) into a resort spanning six decades. [Inside the Story](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/disney-world-2010s/) left off with MagicBands, the Skyliner, new fictional worlds, and another round of plans for EPCOT. By the fiftieth anniversary, some of those plans were ready for visitors.*

## The Florida Project at Fifty

Cinderella Castle wore a gold 50, with golden decorations around its towers. On October 1, 2021, Walt Disney World began celebrating fifty years since its first guests arrived. At EPCOT, Remy's Ratatouille Adventure opened in an expanded France pavilion, where riders could shrink to the size of a rat and scramble through a restaurant kitchen. Disney marked the anniversary with decorations on one landmark and [something new to ride at another park](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/magic-abounds-as-walt-disney-world-resort-begins-50th-anniversary-celebration-301390055.html).

![Cinderella Castle's pink walls and blue towers, wrapped in gold anniversary ribbons, rise above the moat under a blue sky.](oip-image:essays/disney-world-2020s/section-1)

*Cinderella Castle wears its 50th-anniversary decorations on February 1, 2022. Photograph by Benoît Prieur, [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cinderella_Castle_%C3%A0_Magic_Kingdom_(f%C3%A9vrier_2022).JPG).*

The resort entered its sixth decade that day. A person who visited as a child in 1971 was old enough to return with grandchildren. The castle could be an old acquaintance for one member of the family and an astonishing first sight for another.

Roy Disney's opening resort already included hotels, boats, monorails, golf, and places to eat. The decades that followed added parks, rooms, evening entertainment, and more ways to arrange a holiday. Those additions supplied the history told through this series. They also supplied things a visitor might remember ~ a first ride, a favorite hotel, the boat back after a long day.

Keeping those places available required work well beyond opening day. By the fiftieth anniversary, Disney's employees were running the resort after a shutdown that interrupted the ordinary business of welcoming people to Florida. To understand what reached that birthday, we need to step back eighteen months.

## Reopening Disney World: Workers, Cash, and Reservations

Disney World's theme parks [closed on March 16, 2020](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/app/uploads/2021/01/2020-Annual-Report.pdf). The company continued paying employees during the first five weeks of the domestic parks' closure, according to its [May earnings call](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/app/uploads/2020/04/q2-fy20-earnings-transcript.pdf). Furloughs began April 19.

For workers, the terms determined what remained while their jobs were on hold. An April bulletin from the Service Trades Council Union described an agreement covering 43,000 Disney World workers. It preserved seniority and provided continued insurance for eligible covered employees, with Disney paying the premiums for up to twelve months. The union's count described [the workers covered by that agreement](https://www.stcunion.org/wp-content/uploads/04.11.2020-STCU-Victory-Bullet-Points-FINAL-FOR-DISTRIBUTION.pdf), not a tally of permanent job losses.

At the company level, Disney raised cash. Its fiscal 2020 annual report recorded $11.2 billion in net borrowing proceeds. Cash and cash equivalents rose from about $5.4 billion a year earlier to $17.9 billion. Those were resources for the corporation across its businesses, not a financing package earmarked for a Florida ride. Borrowing helped Disney [build a cash reserve during the disruption](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/app/uploads/2021/01/2020-Annual-Report.pdf).

Magic Kingdom and Animal Kingdom reopened on July 11. EPCOT and Hollywood Studios followed on July 15. A ticket alone no longer secured entry on a chosen day. Guests also needed a park reservation, and Disney limited attendance. Face coverings, temperature checks, distancing markers, and changes to vehicle loading became part of the trip. The Skyliner carried [one traveling party per gondola](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/magic-is-here-as-walt-disney-world-resort-theme-parks-prepare-to-welcome-guests-again-301090349.html).

The instructions reached into the hours before arrival. Disney canceled existing dining reservations and moved from a 180-day booking window to sixty days. It suspended FastPass+ and said the additional queue space would help with distancing. A holiday arranged months ahead could require [a fresh set of reservations](https://cdn1.parksmedia.wdprapps.disney.com/media/blog/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/wdru2239480293482093482344.pdf).

Reopening put visitors back in the parks. Construction would give them more to visit.

## EPCOT's Transformation and Disney World's New Rides

Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind opened at EPCOT on May 27, 2022. Its ride vehicles turned as they traveled, directing passengers toward scenes in a story that also sent them along a roller-coaster track. Disney's Imagineers connected the approach to the older Omnimover system, which controlled where riders looked. The new machinery combined that ability with [a coaster's movement and speed](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/how-disney-imagineers-advanced-omnimover-technology-for-guardians-of-the-galaxy-cosmic-rewind/).

![A gold-and-blue spacecraft stands outside Cosmic Rewind, with guests below and Spaceship Earth visible to the left.](oip-image:essays/disney-world-2020s/section-2)

*The exterior of Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind at EPCOT on May 19, 2022, eight days before opening. Photograph by Jedi94, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Guardians_of_the_Galaxy_Cosmic_Rewind_at_EPCOT.jpg).*

The park also added an experience visitors could take at walking pace. Journey of Water, Inspired by Moana [opened on October 16, 2023](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/app/uploads/2024/02/Q1_FY24_Earnings_Presentation.pdf). Its paths and interactive water features put a film character's world into [a landscaped trail](https://disneyexperiences.com/disneyworld-press/release/journey-of-water-inspired-by-moana-continues-wave-of-growth-at-walt-disney-world-2/). The two additions asked different things of the visitor ~ climb into a vehicle and be carried through a story, or walk through a setting and stop along the way.

CommuniCore Hall and Plaza opened in June 2024. Disney described the opening as the [completion of EPCOT's multiyear transformation](https://investors.thewaltdisneycompany.com/files/doc_financials/2024/ar/2024-Annual-Report-1.pdf).

Walt's successors [built EPCOT as a visitor park](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/epcot-center-opening-1982/), with corporate exhibits, rides, and national pavilions. The new additions brought Guardians of the Galaxy and Moana into that visitor park. A person returning to the silver sphere could recognize the landmark while finding another set of things around it.

Magic Kingdom received additions too. [TRON Lightcycle / Run opened on April 4, 2023](https://disneyparksblog.com/wdw/plandisney-official-guide-to-navigating-the-grid-at-tron-lightcycle-run/), and [Tiana's Bayou Adventure followed on June 28, 2024](https://disneyparksblog.com/wdw/new-tiana-wallpapers-to-celebrate-attraction-opening/). New rides supplied a reason to come back. Getting onto them became another part of arranging the day.

## From FastPass+ to Paid Lightning Lane Access

The FASTPASS service described in [The Whole Vacation](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/disney-world-2000s/) let guests reserve a return time without a separate charge for the service. FastPass+ later moved selections into advance planning. After the pandemic suspension, Disney retired FastPass+ and introduced a different arrangement.

Disney Genie launched in Florida on October 19, 2021. The free service offered planning tools and suggested itineraries. Genie+ was a paid addition, announced at $15 per ticket per day at Disney World, that let guests select available arrival times for Lightning Lane entrances at participating attractions. Lightning Lane access at some individual attractions required [a separate purchase](https://d23.com/wish-granted-disney-genie-service-will-plan-your-perfect-day-at-walt-disney-world-resort-and-disneyland-resort/).

Guests could continue using [standby queues or applicable virtual queues](https://d23.com/just-announced-create-your-best-disney-day-when-disney-genie-launches-october-19-at-walt-disney-world-resort/). Paying for Lightning Lane access added another choice to the vacation budget. For a family of four, the announced $15 Genie+ rate meant $60 for one day, before any separately purchased attractions. Buying back some waiting time now had a stated price.

Disney adjusted the arrangements again. From January 9, 2024, guests using date-based tickets [no longer needed a separate park reservation](https://d23.com/disney-d23-rewind-050823/). Other forms of admission could require one.

That July, Lightning Lane Multi Pass replaced Genie+. Visitors could buy it and choose up to three available experiences in one park before arriving. Guests at Disney and certain other hotels could begin selecting seven days ahead of their stay, while others received a three-day window. Separately purchased attractions became Lightning Lane Single Pass. Disney's announcement emphasized [making selections before the trip](https://disneyparksblog.com/wdw/plan-ahead-with-lightning-lane-entry-at-walt-disney-world-starting-july-24/).

These rules reached beyond the queue. Where someone stayed could affect when they planned their rides. The hotel room, ticket, and day in the park belonged to the same holiday, even when they appeared as separate purchases.

## A Holiday Worth Returning To

The resort's first hotels offered a place to sleep near Magic Kingdom. Later additions widened the choice of surroundings, prices, and things to do after dark. [A Longer Stay](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/disney-world-expansion-studios-pleasure-island-hotels/) followed the studio park, Pleasure Island, and the hotel building of the 1980s. The question of what visitors might do between arrival and departure kept producing new projects.

Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser carried that idea into a two-night performance. The package introduced in 2022 included a cabin, meals, activities aboard a fictional spacecraft, and an excursion to Galaxy's Edge. Guests could take roles and make choices within the story. The room was part of the setting, and the schedule included [more than deciding when to go to the park](https://d23.com/7-burning-questions-answered-about-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser/).

![The Galactic Starcruiser atrium has curved white walls, illuminated columns, red bench seats, and a polished floor.](oip-image:essays/disney-world-2020s/section-3)

*The atrium of Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser on August 31, 2022. Photograph by Steven Miller (Sam Howzit), [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/), via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Atrium_(52921221601).jpg).*

The studio park's Great Movie Ride once carried visitors through recreated film scenes. Galaxy's Edge gave them streets to explore. Starcruiser offered the chance to spend two nights inside the production. This time, the audience needed overnight luggage.

The experiment closed in 2023. Disney's year-end earnings release recorded the closure and an [*accelerated depreciation* expense](https://investors.thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/news-details/2023/The-Walt-Disney-Company-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-Earnings-for-Fiscal-2023-11-08-2023/default.aspx) ~ recognizing part of the property's cost as an expense sooner than planned. That accounting expense was not a published total for construction or the venture's cash losses.

Other parts of the whole-vacation offer changed too. Disney's Magical Express stopped serving new arrivals from January 1, 2022. The included airport journey described in installment five gave way to [arrangements travelers made separately](https://plandisney.disney.go.com/question/good-morning-staying-disney-arriving-orlando-airport-jan-479033/). Returning guests could find a familiar hotel while needing a different ride to reach it.

## The Land and Investment Behind the Next Visit

Every version required roads, water, power, and land on which to build. The special district created during the original Florida project supplied a way to organize much of that infrastructure. The working city remained unbuilt, but the resort needed services on the scale of a developed community.

Florida changed the district's government in 2023. The law continued the former Reedy Creek Improvement District as the Central Florida Tourism Oversight District and replaced its board-selection arrangement with five supervisors appointed by the governor and confirmed by the state Senate. Existing debts and valid obligations [remained in place](https://laws.flrules.org/2023/5). Changing the board did not pay off the bonds or remove the need to maintain the utilities.

The district's accounts distinguish debt supported by property taxes from utility debt paid from system revenues. In November 2025, it issued [$169.43 million in utility revenue bonds](https://www.oversightdistrict.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2025-Annual-Financial-Report.pdf), reported in its financial statements the following March. Net utility revenues backed repayment. Disney financed its own business through operating cash, available cash balances, and borrowing, as its [2026 quarterly filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448926000057/dis-20260627.htm) described.

In September 2023, Disney announced a plan to invest roughly $60 billion in its parks and cruise business over about ten years. The plan covered [the business globally](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/disney-plans-to-expand-investment-in-parks-business/), not Florida alone.

The Florida development agreement approved in 2024 described an intention to invest up to $17 billion over ten to twenty years. It also contained a distinct commitment to invest at least $8 billion during the first ten years, including work on existing infrastructure, new development, and technology. The [agreement's terms](https://www.oversightdistrict.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/BOS-Meeting-6-5-24-Agenda-Packet-FINAL.pdf) and [June approval record](https://www.oversightdistrict.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/06-12-24-CFTOD-BOS-Meeting_Meeting-Minutes.pdf) established a program extending beyond the remaining years of the 2020s. These intentions and commitments concerned future spending, not money already spent.

A plan can promise more capacity, an agreement can require investment, and a bond can finance a utility system. Visitors eventually encounter the results as a room, a road, a reliable water supply, or a new place to spend the afternoon.

## Disney World's Next Lands and the Rest of the Decade

By autumn 2026, construction offered another set of possible favorites. At Animal Kingdom, Disney was building Tropical Americas, with attractions based on Encanto and Indiana Jones. Its project register listed [a 2027 opening target](https://disneyexperiences.com/expansions/). The park would continue combining designed landscapes and invented adventures with the living collections introduced in 1998.

At Hollywood Studios, steel was rising for Monstropolis. Disney's August update said parts of the land, including a theatrical experience, shopping, and dining, would open in 2027 while work continued on the suspended door coaster. The distinction kept [the first opening separate from the full project](https://disneyparksblog.com/wdw/monsters-inc-land-monstropolis-walt-disney-world/).

Magic Kingdom's construction program included the Cars setting of Piston Peak and a land devoted to Disney villains. An [August 2026 update](https://disneyparksblog.com/wdw/piston-peak-national-park-builds-on-the-story-of-frontierland/) described work underway, with more of the original park's property being built and rebuilt. Announced targets and active construction left several opening days ahead, with the longer investment program reaching into the following decade.

## Returning With the Next Generation

In an October 1971 photograph, visitors walk along a ramp beneath an elevated monorail. Adults and children occupy the same frame as the machinery. After the land purchases, financing, construction, and opening preparations, there were people going somewhere.

![Visitors walk along a pedestrian ramp beneath the elevated monorail at Walt Disney World in October 1971.](oip-image:essays/walt-disney-world-opening-1971/visitor-transport)

*Visitors and the monorail in October 1971. State Archives of Florida, Florida Department of Commerce collection, [C678601](https://floridamemory.com/items/show/86529). Public domain.*

That photograph appeared in [The Florida Project](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/walt-disney-world-opening-1971/), when the resort's transport system was new. A returning visitor might remember riding the monorail as a child. Decades later, the journey toward the park could be something to share with a child or grandchild boarding for the first time.

Someone might remember Spaceship Earth with Mickey's enormous hand and the red year 2000 above it. Someone else might remember the sphere before the wand. Both could meet beneath it on a later visit.

At [Animal Kingdom](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/animal-kingdom-opening-1998/), animal care and tending the landscape continued after the gates opened. [Pandora and Batuu](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/disney-world-2010s/) added fictional settings to explore. Those places gave a returning visitor more to share, while a younger companion could supply a favorite of their own. A family tradition need not consist entirely of doing what the oldest person remembers.

Some familiar attractions needed their own return. The Walt Disney World Railroad resumed carrying guests in December 2022. Disney's account of the reopening introduced John, a railroad cast member whose grandfather and great-grandfather worked as steam-train engineers in Pittsburgh's steel mills. He described children seeing a train round the corner and [hearing its whistle for the first time](https://disneyparksblog.com/wdw/carrying-on-a-legacy-the-walt-disney-world-railroad-returns/).

That was a pleasure available on an opening-day attraction more than fifty years after the opening. Maintenance and operating work made it available again. A child did not need to know the date of the first departure to enjoy this one.

Friends, couples, and adults making their first visit can begin their own collection of favorites. Across the parks and hotels, the machinery of a large resort makes room for a small occasion ~ a ride together, a shared laugh, a photograph worth keeping.

For someone returning with children or grandchildren, the next trip can begin with a familiar view of the castle. They may know which way to turn, where to board the boat, and which ride they want to share first. Then comes the pleasure of taking someone who knows none of it through the gates.

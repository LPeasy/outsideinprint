## A Note from Robert V. Ussley

The pieces this week ask a plain question: Where does your attention belong? One piece stops guessing what other people think. Another opens a clenched hand. A third looks past the wallet and counts love, rest, and useful work. The last follows a walker who turns the corner before the street shows what comes next. The lesson is practical. Own your choices, keep your promises, and listen when someone tells you the truth. Let other people carry their own weather. You cannot choose every result, but the next move is yours.

## New from Outside In Print

### I Make Myself

Responsibility starts with the next choice. This affirmation keeps your attention close to home: on your promises, your actions, and how you respond when plans go wrong.

Read: [I Make Myself](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/i-make-myself/)

### Rich in Spirit

A wallet can count money, but it cannot count love, useful work, rest, thanks, or help from a friend. Wealth needs a wider measure.

Read: [Rich in Spirit](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/rich-in-spirit/)

### Open Hands

A clenched hand cannot receive much. This affirmation asks you to choose, knock on the door, and then stop throwing your whole body against it.

Read: [Open Hands](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/open-hands/)

### Pay Attention to You

Trying to read other people's minds pulls you out of your own life. This affirmation brings your attention back to the ground under your feet and the next move you can make.

Read: [Pay Attention to You](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/pay-attention-to-you/)

## This Week's Virtue

**Industry**

Industry begins with the work that is yours, when it is time to do it. Put down the phone, make the call, open the notebook, and take the walk. Leave other people's thoughts with them. A day takes shape when your attention and your work are in the same place.

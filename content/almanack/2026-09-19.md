## This Week's Virtue

**Humility**

Humility begins when you let another person finish. A story can turn on a detail you did not know, and a number can hide the people it leaves out. Speak plainly and admit where the record stops. The pause gives you room to learn before you decide.

> Being ignorant is not so much a Shame, as being unwilling to learn.
>
> ~ Poor Richard

## In the Margins

### One Number

**$87,460**

Census put real median U.S. household income at $87,460 for 2025, the highest in its series since 1967. The measure adjusts for inflation but counts money before taxes. Source: [U.S. Census Bureau](https://www.census.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026/income-poverty-health-insurance-coverage.html)

### One Document

**U.S. Postal Service: Cost-Cutting Initiatives and Other Factors Have Contributed to Declining Service Performance**

Issued by: U.S. Government Accountability Office, 2026-09-17.

GAO says USPS lengthened expected delivery for some First-Class Mail by one to two days. It has missed most of its lowered service targets since fiscal 2021. Source: [U.S. Government Accountability Office](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-108527)

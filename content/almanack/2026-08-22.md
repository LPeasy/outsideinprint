## This Week's Virtue

**Sincerity**

Sincerity starts with the part that is yours. Name the failed plan, the broken cup, or the apology without turning it into a performance. Speak plainly, repair what you can, and leave another person's answer to them. Honest action needs fewer excuses and less rehearsal.

### One Document

**B-338306: Bears Ears National Monument Resource Management Plan**

Issued by: U.S. Government Accountability Office, 2026-08-12.

The Government Accountability Office found that the Bears Ears plan counts as a rule under the Congressional Review Act. It must be submitted to Congress and the Comptroller General before it can take effect. Source: [U.S. Government Accountability Office](https://www.gao.gov/products/b-338306)

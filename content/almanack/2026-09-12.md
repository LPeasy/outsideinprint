## This Week's Virtue

**Sincerity**

Sincerity is more than telling the truth when someone asks. It means speaking without a hidden trap and acting for the reason you give. A kind lie may spare a moment, but it shifts the cost to someone else. Say less if you must, and make sure your words can bear the facts.

### One Number

**$12.8 trillion**

U.S. household and nonprofit net worth rose by $12.8 trillion in the second quarter, mainly because stock values climbed. That was an asset gain, not the same as new cash income. Source: [Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/z1/current/recent_developments.htm)

### One Document

**Disaster Risk: Improvements Needed to Enhance FEMA’s National Risk Index**

Issued by: U.S. Government Accountability Office, 2026-09-10.

GAO found three gaps in FEMA’s National Risk Index: guidance for combining its scores with other tools, consistent sensitivity tests, and steady user feedback. It made three recommendations. Source: [U.S. Government Accountability Office](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-107894)

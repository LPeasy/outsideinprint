## A Note from Robert V. Ussley

This week begins with a bend in the path. The road does not show its whole length. The river does not place every stone in view. An open hand cannot hold yesterday and tomorrow at once. Four new affirmations put choice back in the present: decide who you are becoming, start before certainty arrives, pay attention to the body carrying you, and let old weight go when it has no work left to do. The road may remain hard. A visible next step gives you something to take, test, and correct after your foot lands.

## This Week's Virtue

**Tranquillity**

Tranquillity travels on a bending road. Feel the stone under your foot and let the water settle after the pebble falls. Notice what changed without handing the whole day to it. Calm leaves enough of your attention free for the next choice and the adjustment that may follow.

## In the Margins

### One Number

**3.4%**

Consumer prices for all urban consumers rose 3.4 percent over the 12 months ending in July, before seasonal adjustment. In July alone, the index rose 0.1 percent after seasonal adjustment; shelter made up about two-thirds of that monthly increase. Source: [U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.nr0.htm)

### One Document

**Differential Pay for Prescribed Wildland Fire Activities**

Issued by: U.S. Office of Personnel Management, 2026-08-14.

The final rule adds prescribed wildland-fire work to activities covered by hazardous-duty or environmental-differential pay. It authorizes a 25 percent differential for General Schedule and Federal Wage System employees on firefighting crews whose fireline work directly carries out or controls prescribed burns. Source: [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/14/2026-16687/differential-pay-for-prescribed-wildland-fire-activities)

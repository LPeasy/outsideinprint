## A Note from Robert V. Ussley

A paper bird can open back into a sheet. A promise gets proof when your feet touch the floor. A hard week can change when you choose the next step instead of letting the week decide for you. This issue looks at the words we repeat when no one else can hear. Words may help, but they cannot keep a promise, take a walk, or mend a life. Only action can do that: get up, keep going, drink the water, make the call, or start again. Say it plainly, then do one thing today that makes the words true.

## This Week's Virtue

**Tranquillity**

A failed plan, a hard morning, or a worried body can put you on trial in your own mind. Stop the trial, see what happened, and learn what you can. Then choose the next good step. Tranquillity does not erase pain or excuse harm; it keeps one bad day from taking charge of the next one. Put your feet on the floor and continue.

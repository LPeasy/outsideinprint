## This Week's Virtue

**Resolution**

Resolution turns spoken intention into conduct. Decide what belongs to you, then do it without theater or delay. Keep the promise small enough to carry and firm enough to count. A practiced sentence can steady the mind, but only the next chosen act gives it weight. Resolve, perform, correct, and begin again tomorrow.

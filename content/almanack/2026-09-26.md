**Hepatitis A Outbreak Associated with Cuba — Florida, June 2024–February 2026**

Issued by: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention and Florida Department of Health, 2026-09-24.

The report identified 79 Florida cases that met its Cuba-travel-associated outbreak definition. Seventy-six patients reported recent Cuba travel. Investigators could not confirm every infection happened there. Source: [Morbidity and Mortality Weekly Report](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/75/wr/mm7537a1.htm)

## Worth Reprinting

### [Nothing to See Here](https://outsideinprint.org/essays/nothing-to-see-here/)

Lyme disease was not imagined. Bad tests, narrow case definitions, official caution, insurance pressure, secrecy, and ridicule made it smaller inside the public record.

Lyme disease is real, yet the public count often made it look small. This essay shows how weak tests, tight case rules, pressure from insurers, secrecy, and mockery shut sick people out. We bring it back because a bad count can close the file while the patient stays ill.

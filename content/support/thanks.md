You’re back from Square checkout. Thank you for supporting Outside In Print. Your support helps us publish independent books, essays, and public-interest work.

Check your Square receipt email for confirmation:

- **One-time support:** The receipt confirms the amount charged.
- **Monthly support:** The receipt confirms the $5 monthly subscription and includes the link to manage or cancel it.

If a receipt does not arrive, check your spam or junk folder. You can [return to the support options](/support/) or [contact Outside In Print](/contact/) for help.

Returning to this page does not by itself confirm that payment succeeded. Your Square receipt is the authoritative confirmation.

Do not send a full card number, card-security code, password, or account-recovery code.

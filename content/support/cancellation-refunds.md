These terms apply to voluntary reader-support payments made through Outside In Print.

## Support options

The storefront may offer:

- one-time support in a whole-dollar amount from $5 to $500; and
- fixed support of $5 per month.

Support does not include a book, membership benefit, editorial influence, or special access. Book purchases use separate checkout pages.

## Monthly renewal

A monthly payment is charged on the day it is started and automatically renews each month until canceled. Square sends a receipt and subscription-management link to the email address entered at checkout.

The selected amount and monthly cadence appear before checkout is completed. A card issuer may apply its own currency-conversion or foreign-transaction charge to an international card.

## Cancellation

Cancel through the management link in a Square receipt email. Cancellation takes effect for the next billing cycle when completed before that renewal is processed. Canceling does not automatically refund an earlier payment.

If the management link is unavailable, email [support@outsideinprint.org](mailto:support@outsideinprint.org) with the Square transaction or subscription identifier and the email address used at checkout. Do not send card information.

## Refunds

Outside In Print will refund the most recent one-time or monthly support payment when requested within seven days after the charge. Older monthly cycles are not prorated or refunded merely because the subscription is later canceled.

Duplicate, unauthorized, or erroneous charges may be refunded after investigation regardless of that courtesy window, as required by applicable law and payment-network rules. Approved refunds return to the original payment method; Square, the card network, and the financial institution control final posting time.

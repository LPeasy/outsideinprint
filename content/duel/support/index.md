Duel is an iPhone-only, local phone-to-phone game from Outside in Print LLC.

For help, email [support@outsideinprint.org](mailto:support@outsideinprint.org). Never send your Apple Account password, verification code, payment-card information, full receipt, identity documents, or other sensitive financial information.

## Compatibility

Duel requires:

- An iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
- Two physical iPhones running the same current Duel version for a local match.
- Nearby Interaction support for precise distance and direction on both phones.
- Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Local Network access, Nearby Interaction access, and Motion access.
- An internet connection for Game Center and App Store purchases.

Duel does not use the camera. Simulator and iPad behavior do not prove that a phone supports the full local sensing path. Use Duel’s **Capability Check** on each iPhone before pairing.

## First-time setup

1. Install the current Duel version on both iPhones.
2. Turn on Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.
3. Open Duel and allow Local Network, Nearby Interaction, Bluetooth, and Motion access when requested.
4. Choose **Enter Game Center** for competitive play, or **Continue Offline** for local unranked play.
5. Start a local duel and select **Run Check** on both phones.

If access was denied, open the iPhone Settings app, allow the affected permission for Duel, then restart pairing or calibration.

## Pair two iPhones

1. Keep both phones unlocked, nearby, and on the Pairing screen.
2. Select **Start Pairing** on both phones.
3. Wait for peer discovery, connection, token exchange, Nearby Interaction, direction, and distance to become ready.
4. Continue only after both phones report a ready path.

If pairing stalls:

- Confirm both phones use the same Duel version.
- Confirm Wi-Fi and Bluetooth remain enabled.
- Recheck Local Network and Nearby Interaction permissions on both phones.
- Exit pairing on both phones and start again.
- Relaunch Duel on both phones if the connection remains stale.
- Run Capability Check again if direction or precise distance stays unavailable. Unsupported hardware cannot provide the full Duel sensing path.

## Safety and calibration

Use a clear area with room around both players. Keep a secure grip on the phone, use controlled movement, and stop if either player feels unsafe.

Hold each phone upright in portrait and steady, then select **Run Calibration**. If calibration fails:

- Allow Motion access in iPhone Settings.
- Hold the phone upright and reduce hand movement.
- Run calibration again on the physical iPhone.
- Restart the duel if motion samples remain unavailable.

The target is the peer phone. Duel does not use camera-based scoring.

## Game Center and offline play

Game Center provides player identity, live leaderboard reads, and submission of qualifying ranked wins. A competitive result is submitted only when hardware, pairing, calibration, tracking, confidence, and winner checks all pass.

You may choose **Continue Offline** for local play. Local results can remain stored on that iPhone, but Game Center standing and competitive leaderboard submission are unavailable. Offline or failed submissions are not queued for later posting.

For Game Center trouble, confirm the iPhone is signed in to Game Center, internet access works, and account restrictions do not block gaming services. You can continue in offline unranked mode while Game Center is unavailable.

## Paid cosmetic sets

Duel’s launch paid products are one-time, non-consumable cosmetic sets:

- **White Hat Set:** Townline Six, White Hat Banner, Sunset Ledger, and Brass Stamp.

These items change presentation only. They do not improve aim, scoring, confidence, Gold, Reputation, Standing, or leaderboard position. The launch paid products do not sell Gold.

Prices are displayed and confirmed by Apple in your local currency. Outside in Print LLC does not receive your payment credentials.

## Purchase a set

Open **Outfitter**, choose a set, and select the button showing the App Store price. Apple presents the confirmation sheet. Duel adds the cosmetics to the **Saddle Locker** only after Apple verifies the transaction.

If the purchase is cancelled or cannot be verified, no cosmetics are granted.

## Restore purchases

Use Restore Purchases after reinstalling Duel, changing devices, or when a previously purchased set is missing:

1. Connect to the internet.
2. Confirm the App Store is using the same Apple Account that made the purchase.
3. Open **Outfitter** and select **Restore Purchases**.
4. Complete any Apple authentication request.
5. Check the Saddle Locker. If needed, select **Refresh Board** or relaunch Duel.

Do not buy the set again while troubleshooting a missing entitlement.

## Purchase troubleshooting

### The price or Buy button is unavailable

- Check internet access and [Apple’s service status](https://www.apple.com/support/systemstatus/).
- Confirm the App Store is signed in.
- Check Screen Time or device-management restrictions for in-app purchases.
- Select **Refresh Board** and try again later.

### The purchase says Pending Approval

Apple has not yet supplied a verified transaction. This can occur with Ask to Buy or another interrupted or pending App Store transaction. Complete any required approval or billing action. Duel unlocks the set only after Apple verifies it. After Apple confirms the purchase, use **Restore Purchases** if the set remains missing.

### Apple charged me, but the cosmetics are missing

Check your [Apple purchase history](https://support.apple.com/118212), confirm the purchasing Apple Account, then use **Restore Purchases**. If Apple shows a completed purchase and restore still fails, email Duel Support with the set name, approximate date, device model, iOS version, app version, and exact error message. Do not attach a full receipt.

### Purchases are disabled

Review Apple’s [in-app purchase restriction guidance](https://support.apple.com/102470). Screen Time, family controls, or device management may prevent purchases.

## Refunds

Apple controls App Store billing and refund decisions. Submit an eligible request through [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/) and follow [Apple’s refund guidance](https://support.apple.com/118223). A pending charge may need to finish before Apple accepts a refund request.

If Apple refunds or revokes a set, Duel may remove its cosmetics from the Saddle Locker. Duel Support can troubleshoot entitlement display, but cannot approve or accelerate an Apple refund.

## Data and privacy

Read the [Duel Privacy Policy](/duel/privacy/).

Duel stores derived local match results, confidence and distance categories, summarized angular error, local progression, settings, and purchase-entitlement state. It does not persist raw Motion or Nearby Interaction streams, audio, or video. Game Center and App Store transactions are processed through Apple. Duel currently has no custom player-account or remote-match backend.

Deleting Duel can remove local-only history, settings, Gold, and non-App-Store progression. Restorable App Store cosmetics and Game Center records are handled separately through Apple, so reinstalling should not be the first troubleshooting step.

## Escalate an issue

Email [support@outsideinprint.org](mailto:support@outsideinprint.org) with the subject **Duel Support — Pairing**, **Duel Support — Purchase**, **Duel Support — Game Center**, or **Duel Support — Other**.

Include:

- iPhone model and iOS version.
- Duel version, if known.
- Both phone models for a pairing issue.
- The exact onscreen message.
- What you expected, what happened, and steps that reproduce it.
- For purchases, the set name, approximate purchase date, whether Apple issued a receipt, and the result of Restore Purchases.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment details, identity documents, or unredacted account screenshots.

This policy applies to paperback books purchased directly from Outside In Print. Kindle purchases remain subject to Amazon's terms.

## Where direct paperbacks ship

Direct paperback checkout accepts eligible delivery addresses in the 50 U.S. states and the District of Columbia. International shipping, expedited service, and web pickup are not offered in the first storefront release. Orders are limited to six books.

## Shipping charges

Outside In Print uses USPS Media Mail for standard book orders:

- one book: $4.99;
- two or three books: $5.99; and
- four to six books: $7.49.

Mandatory shipping charges are included in the taxable amount on Florida orders when required. Shipping rates may be revised prospectively if packed weight or carrier charges change. The amount shown at checkout governs the order.

## Handling and delivery

Sellable in-stock books ordinarily leave Outside In Print within two business days after completed payment. Weekends, federal holidays, severe weather, carrier disruption, inventory errors, and payment review can extend this period. Carrier transit estimates are not delivery guarantees.

The customer is responsible for providing a complete, deliverable address. Contact [support@outsideinprint.org](mailto:support@outsideinprint.org) immediately after checkout if an address is wrong. An address cannot be changed after the package enters the carrier network. Additional postage caused by an incorrect customer-supplied address may be deducted from a refund or charged before reshipment where lawful.

## Damage, loss, or wrong item

Report a damaged, lost, or incorrect shipment within 14 days after the recorded delivery date, or within 14 days after the expected delivery date when tracking does not show delivery. Include the Square order number and, for damage or a wrong item, clear photographs of the package and book. Do not send card information.

Outside In Print will investigate and, when the claim is confirmed, replace the book if inventory permits or refund the affected item and applicable original shipping. Outside In Print pays reasonable return postage for its own fulfillment error or a confirmed damaged item when a return is requested.

## Voluntary returns

An unused paperback in resaleable condition may be returned within 30 days after delivery. Email support first for return instructions. The customer pays return postage. The book must be protected in transit and remain free from writing, stains, odors, bent covers, water damage, or other customer-caused damage.

After the return is inspected and accepted, Outside In Print refunds the returned book price and associated sales tax to the original payment method. Original shipping is not refunded for a voluntary return. A copy that is not resaleable will not be restored to inventory and may receive a reduced or denied refund to the extent permitted by law.

## Cancellations

Request cancellation immediately. An unshipped order may be canceled and refunded. A packed or shipped order follows the return rules above.

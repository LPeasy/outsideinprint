## The people behind the imprint

Robert V. Ussley publishes and edits Outside In Print. He writes much of what you'll read here, including the Syd and Oliver dialogues, and designs and develops the site.

Contributing writers bring their own experience, knowledge, and ways of seeing. Their work appears alongside Robert's, with each writer's byline attached to the piece. [The Outside Byline](/collections/the-outside-byline/) gathers guest essays in one place.

You can [meet Robert and browse his writing](/authors/robert-v-ussley/). The small wandering Bob on the page is the site's companion; his controls offer music and reading suggestions.

## How we approach the work

Reported pieces begin with records, sources, and questions worth testing. We follow the evidence, examine incentives and tradeoffs, and ask what decisions mean for the people who live with them. Judgments need reasons; uncertainty deserves to be named.

Source links let you examine the record for yourself. Essays distinguish the writer's interpretation from what the evidence establishes. Fiction, dialogues, and personal reflections have their own forms and purposes.

If you spot a factual error or have an editorial question, [get in touch](/contact/). Include the piece and the passage so we can check it.

## Beyond the essays

Illustration is part of how OIP tells a story. The [Gallery](/gallery/) brings the artwork together, with links back to the writing. The [Bookstore](/shop/) makes room for longer fiction and nonfiction.

There's also room to play and listen: visit the [Games](/games/) or put something on in [Music](/music/). Humor, pictures, and a good tune belong here alongside the questions that take longer to answer.

For a subject to follow, [browse the collections](/collections/). To find a particular piece, [search the library](/library/).

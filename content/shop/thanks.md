Expect your secure EPUB download link at the delivery email address you entered during checkout.

Square is confirming your payment. After Square confirms the completed payment, Outside In Print will email your private download link. It usually arrives within a few minutes.

If the message does not appear, check your spam or junk folder. If it is still missing after 15 minutes, [contact Outside In Print](/contact/) for help. You can also [return to the bookstore](/shop/).

Reaching this page by itself does not confirm that payment succeeded. Your Square receipt and EPUB delivery email confirm completion.

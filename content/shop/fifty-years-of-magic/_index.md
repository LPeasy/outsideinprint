## About the book

A child sees a castle. A parent checks the ticket. Somewhere behind the holiday stand years of drawings, construction, borrowing, and decisions about what a family might want to do next.

*Fifty Years of Magic* follows Walt Disney World from Walt's Florida plans and Roy's work bringing them to opening through EPCOT, the studio park, Animal Kingdom, and the hotels and journeys around them. Annual reports, planning papers, period photographs, and contemporary accounts reveal how the resort grew, what it cost to build, and how Disney kept finding reasons for visitors to return.

Robert V. Ussley brings the business history down to the scale of a holiday ~ a room for the night, a boat across the water, another day worth staying. The story reaches October 2026, with new lands ahead and another generation waiting at the gates.

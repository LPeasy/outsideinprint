# Musings Series Contract

Musings is Outside In Print's short-form source-free reflection lane. It holds brief personal observations, questions, recognitions, and invitations to attention. It is not an essay, report, research note, or public-claim package.

## What Belongs Here

- First-person reflection and ordinary lived experience.
- A single thought, image, question, or small practical recognition.
- Short blurbs with no fixed word count, no required section heading, and no required visual.
- Plain language that leaves room for the reader instead of trying to prove a large conclusion.

## Voice And Pace

A Musing should sound like one person talking honestly to another, not a committee-polished essay. The family resemblance is direct, familiar, and willing to be blunt. A piece may be warm, sharp, funny, rough-edged, image-driven, or quiet as the thought requires. This guidance is not a required sequence or validator checklist. Do not smooth a strong authorial voice into generic calm reflection.

“Say Thanks” is one strong model for directness: a concrete human moment, quoted speech, one earned blunt line, and repetition that carries the close. Treat that control and presence as an example, not a template.

- Begin close to a lived moment, object, image, question, or direct recognition when that serves the thought.
- First person and direct address are both welcome.
- Use ordinary words, contractions, short sentences, and short paragraphs when they fit the voice.
- Concrete objects, actions, quoted speech, and earned imagery often carry more than explanation.
- Let repetition carry emphasis when it sounds natural.
- A real limit or honest distinction can keep counsel from turning into a promise.
- Blunt imperatives may belong. Profanity may remain when it is authentic and proportionate; neither is required.
- Stop when the thought lands. A recognition, image, question, or usable command may close the piece. Do not append an automatic recap.

Avoid essay scaffolding, formal transitions, therapeutic jargon, workshop language, ornament that hides the thought, and over-explaining. Imagery and metaphor are welcome when they reveal the thought. Rawness belongs in the cadence, not in preventable errors. Proofread new work without sanding away the writer's edge. This voice guidance is prospective; it does not require published Musings to follow one voice or sequence.

## Authorial Freedom And Claim Boundary

Musings is Outside In Print's most open authorial lane. A Musing may use unqualified personal, spiritual, existential, philosophical, moral, or aspirational language. It may speak in the first person or a direct universal voice. It may offer counsel, hope, blessing, warning, or a vision of the good without being recast as a hedge, a diagnosis, a research claim, or an evidence-bound argument.

Broad or certain language does not become an external factual claim merely because it is declarative. An author does not need to add disclaimers such as "I think," "in my view," or "for me" to a personal conclusion, spiritual assurance, or life observation. Such writing needs no citations, research package, source checklist, or OIP-99 report.

The `SOURCE_FREE` declaration has one narrow job: it means the Musing makes no concrete external factual claim. Its collection must be either `musings` or an approved source-free series governed by its own contract. What You Tell Yourself is the first approved derived series. If a Musing makes an empirical, historical, current, legal, medical, statistical, scientific, or named-actor claim about the world, record appropriate sources and evidence control for that claim. It may remain a Musing when reflection remains its primary form; move it to another OIP lane only when it becomes chiefly reporting, analysis, or a public-claim package.

Do not add a named source merely to decorate a personal reflection. A site integration may carry a compact internal editorial record when useful, but it must describe the actual claim posture rather than invent evidence requirements for an author's voice.

## Authoring And Publication

New entries belong under content/essays/musings/ so they remain in the archive and Library. Use explicit front matter with a title, date, draft state, concise public description, section_label: "Musing", library_type: "musing", and either collections: ["musings"] or the single approved collection declared by a derived source-free series contract.

The source-free exception requires both source_mode: "SOURCE_FREE" and external_factual_claims: "none". A public Musing needs either a social image or a documented image_exempt reason, but no visual is otherwise required.

Musings are exempt from the essay-specific OIP-99 package only while all of those source-free declarations are accurate. Fully declared source-free Musings are also exempt from the adverbial `still` house-style guardrail so personal cadence can remain intact. Site integrations that normally require OIP-99 evidence, including editorial-cartoon essay links, must honor the same exact source-free Musing predicate rather than forcing the item to appear standalone. That narrow exception does not extend to public-claim work. Standard site, accessibility, and all other house-style checks remain in force. If an external factual claim becomes central, stop treating the piece as a Musing and reopen the appropriate evidence and editorial controls.

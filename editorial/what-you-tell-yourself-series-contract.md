# What You Tell Yourself Series Contract

What You Tell Yourself is an open-ended Outside In Print series about affirmations, identity, and action. Entries publish one at a time when they are ready.

## Source

Each entry begins with at least one affirmation from [affirmations-bank.md](affirmations-bank.md). The chosen affirmation appears as at least one canonical `franklin-pullquote` in the published piece.

Record each entry and its affirmation in the bank's use log.

## Form

The series uses the source-free short-reflection form defined in [musings-series-contract.md](musings-series-contract.md).

Each entry:

- belongs to `collections: ["what-you-tell-yourself"]`;
- uses `section_label: "Musing"` and `library_type: "musing"`;
- uses `source_mode: "SOURCE_FREE"` and `external_factual_claims: "none"` only when those declarations remain accurate;
- stays plain, personal, and grounded in an action, experience, or recognition;
- includes at least one affirmation pull quote;
- avoids turning an affirmation into a medical, financial, scientific, or historical claim.

The series is source-free eligible under the same narrow evidence exception as Musings. If a concrete external factual claim enters a piece, reopen the appropriate evidence controls before publication.
